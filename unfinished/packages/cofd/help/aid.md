+AID  -- First aid (Dexterity + Medicine).

SYNTAX
  +aid <target>           Treat the named character.
  +aid                    Self-aid (if damaged).
  +aid/reset <player>     Staff: clear scene lock.

PERMISSIONS
  Self: connected. Other / reset: canEdit.

MECHANICS
  Pool Dex+Medicine (−3 untrained). Wound penalty
  applies. Per success: lethal→bashing, then clear
  bashing. No agg. Exceptional also clears 1 bashing.
  Dramatic failure: +1 lethal. Once per scene/patient.
