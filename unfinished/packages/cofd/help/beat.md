+BEAT  -- Award, subtract, or view Beats (5 → 1 XP).

SYNTAX
  +beat                              View your pools.
  +beat <player>                     View another.
  +beat/add [<p>] [= <reason>]       Award 1 Beat.
  +beat/sub [<p>] [= <reason>]       Subtract 1 Beat.
  +beat/add/arcane [<p>] [= reason]  Arcane Beat.
  +beat/sub/arcane [<p>] [= reason]  Subtract Arcane.

SWITCHES
  /add /sub     Standard track (never below 0).
  /arcane       With /add or /sub: Arcane track.

PERMISSIONS
  Self: connected. Other: canEdit (builder+).
