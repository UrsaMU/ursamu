+MANTLE

  Seasonal Court Mantle (CtL p.117–118).

SYNTAX
  +mantle                 Show dots and bonuses.
  +mantle/glamour <note>  +1 Glamour (1/scene).
  +mantle/debt            Autumn•••• Debt wipe (1/story).
  +mantle/clarity         Spring••••• Clarity convert.
  +sheet/set mantle:spring=3

DICE (auto on +roll)
  Spring  seduce, indulge, teamwork
  Summer  intimidate; /defend attacks
  Autumn  Occult/fae; fear
  Winter  Stealth/spy; Subterfuge

HIGH-DOT
  Summer•••   +roll/protect armor = Mantle
  Summer••••• +attack/defend → aggravated
  Autumn•••  Contract −1G vs Fae (on target)
  Autumn•••• +mantle/debt
  Spring••••• +mantle/clarity
  Winter••••• ignore wound pen; +phys when hurt
