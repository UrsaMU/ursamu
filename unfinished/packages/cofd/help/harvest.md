+HARVEST

  Draw Glamour from strong emotion (CtL pp.103–104).
  Cannot harvest fae (Lost, hobgoblins, True Fae).

SYNTAX
  +harvest [target][=pool]
  +harvest/reap <target>
  +reap <target>

RULES
  Default pool Presence+Empathy. Each success = 1 Glamour
  (capped at your maximum). Reaping fills Glamour fully,
  drains Willpower equal to Wyrd, applies Ravaged, and is
  a Clarity breaking point (+integrity/break).

EXAMPLES
  +harvest
  +harvest Alice
  +harvest Bob=Manipulation+Expression
  +harvest/reap Charlie

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help bedlam, +help integrity
