+KENNING  -- Fae perception (Wits + Wyrd).

**+kenning** rolls Wits+Wyrd to sense magic, harvest
suitability, or fae kin. Requires a changeling sheet.

SYNTAX
  +kenning                   Area / general kenning.
  +kenning <target>          Read one person.

NOTES
  No target: tiered sense of nearby magic (presence).
  Mortal: harvest suitability. Lost: fae recognition.
  Other templates: glamour signature. Exceptional may
  name nature (ST). Dramatic failure misleads (ST).

EXAMPLES
  +kenning
  +kenning Alice

SEE ALSO: help perception, help shift, help changeling
