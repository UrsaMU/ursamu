+BEDLAM

  Incite Bedlam — unleash raw Arcadian emotion (CtL p.110).
  Costs 1 Glamour + 1 Willpower. Contests everyone who can
  sense you (PCs in the room with sheets).

SYNTAX
  +bedlam <emotion> [extra Glamour 0-5]

EMOTIONS
  desire / spring   → Wanton
  wrath / summer    → Competitive
  fear / autumn     → Frightened
  sorrow / winter   → Lethargic

RULES
  Pool Manipulation + Wyrd (+Pandemoniacal, +extra G dice).
  Targets contest with Composure + Wyrd. Exceptional success
  adds Spooked. Cannot spare allies.

EXAMPLES
  +bedlam fear
  +bedlam desire 2

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help harvest, +help condition
