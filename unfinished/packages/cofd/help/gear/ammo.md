+GEAR AMMO  -- Magazines and ammunition stacks.

STACKING
  /add merges same-key stacks. Full reload destroy
  empty stack. Floor get may leave separate stacks.

SPLITTING
  +gear/split <slot>=<n>  (1 ≤ n < count)

CONCEALMENT
  Dark mags show [concealed] in +gear.

EXAMPLES
  +gear/add magazine-9mm-light
  +gear/split 3=1
  give 4=Marcus
