+GEAR INVENTORY  -- Slots, add/remove, equip.

COMMANDS
  +gear/add <key>[/<note>] [for <p>]
  +gear/remove <#>
  +gear/equip <#>
  +gear/unequip weapon|armor

EFFECTS
  Equipped armor → Defense/Speed on sheet.
  Equipped weapon → +roll/weapon and +attack.
