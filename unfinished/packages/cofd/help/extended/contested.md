+EXTENDED CONTESTED  -- Race two extended actions.

SETUP
  Both actions active, then staff links:
    +extended/contest <idA>+<idB>

RESOLUTION
  First side past target succeeds; sibling abandoned.
  /finish also abandons sibling. Exhausted max-rolls
  does **not** auto-end the other side.

NOTES
  One contest per action; contestId on both records.
