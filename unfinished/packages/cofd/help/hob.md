+HOB

  Hobgoblins — Hedge denizens (CtL p.252 light).

SYNTAX
  +hob                      Status (hobgoblin sheet).
  +hob/concepts             Concept list.
  +hob/powers               Dread Power keys.
  +hob/power <key>          Activate a known power.

STAFF
  +hob/create <p>=Name/concept[/wyrd]
  +hob/grant <p>=<power-key>

CONCEPTS
  trickster  merchant  predator  guardian  crafter  custom

NOTES
  Hobgoblins have Wyrd, Glamour, frailties, Dread Powers.
  NPC archetype: hobgoblin-trader (+npc/build).

EXAMPLES
  +hob/create Bob=Needlegrin/merchant/3
  +hob/power innocuous

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help market, +help npc
