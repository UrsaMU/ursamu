+FRENZY  -- Resist or ride the Beast (VtR).

SYNTAX
  +frenzy/status                      Current frenzy state.
  +frenzy/resist <kind> [+/-N]        Resolve+Composure roll.
  +frenzy/ride <kind>                 Ride the Wave (1 WP).
  +frenzy/enter <kind>                Enter frenzy (no roll).
  +frenzy/end                         End frenzy.

KINDS
  hunger   Low Vitae / scent of blood.
  anger    Insult, assault, threatened Touchstone.
  terror   Fire, sunlight, final death (Rötschreck).

Pool uses the Humanity rating modifier. Failure applies the
Frenzied Condition (+2 Physical, ignore wounds).
