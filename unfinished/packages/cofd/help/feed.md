+FEED  -- Drink blood to regain Vitae (VtR).

SYNTAX
  +feed animal [N]              Animal blood (BP 1 only).
  +feed human [N]               Kine blood.
  +feed kindred [N]             Vampire vitae.
  +feed <player>[=N]            Feed on a character.
  +feed/human 2                 Switch form of source.

Amount defaults to 1. Player vessels take lethal damage
equal to Vitae taken. High BP may refuse animal blood.

SEE ALSO: help vitae, help integrity, help vampire
