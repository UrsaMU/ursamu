+FETCH

  Fae doubles and Echoes (CtL p.233+). Fetches have Wyrd
  and Glamour but no Contracts, seeming, or kith.

SYNTAX
  +fetch                    Status (changeling link or fetch).
  +fetch/echoes             Echo catalog.
  +fetch/echo <slug> [note] Activate Echo (fetch sheet).
  +fetch/flaw <text>        Human flaw.
  +fetch/met                Met original face-to-face.

STAFF
  +fetch/create <ch>=<Name>[/flaw][/mat] [for <player>]
  +fetch/link <changeling>=<fetch player>
  +fetch/mode <p>=adversary|other-half|hard-lesson
  +fetch/grant-echo <p>=<slug>

ECHOES (need Wyrd)
  attuned (auto) normalcy heart-of-wax enter-hedge
  summon-shard mimic-contract shadow-boxing shadow-step
  death-of-glamour call-huntsmen

NOTES
  Normalcy ON blocks other Echoes (drop with /echo normalcy).
  Mimic Contract requires +fetch/met first.
  NPC archetype: fetch-double (+npc).

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help clash, +help npc
