+COMBAT/ATTACK

See also: +help combat (overview)

SYNTAX
  +attack[/<mode>] <target> [+upgrade|+glitch]
  +attack <ds> · +reload [snap]

MODES
  aim burst auto pb melee fastdraw knife
  sg · sg-close · sg-pb

EXAMPLES
  +attack 12
  +attack/aim eswat
  +attack/burst Alice +upgrade
  +attack horde
  +reload
  wield p226
  +attack/auto goon 1

SEE ALSO: +help combat/examples
