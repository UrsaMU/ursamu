+COMBAT/TACTICS

See also: +help combat (overview)

SYNTAX
  +range [m] · +range/guide
  +horde/spawn <n> [name] · /clear
  +suppress <zone-m> <ds>[,ds…]
  +grenade [open|confined]
  +scene[/tick] · +flavor [on|off]

EXAMPLES
  +range 15
  +horde/spawn 8 street punks
  +attack horde
  +suppress 10 12,14,10
  +grenade open
  +scene/tick
