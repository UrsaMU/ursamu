+GNAME

Set a gradient moniker across the letters of your name.

SYNTAX
  +gname <color1> <color2> [<color3> ...]
  +gname reset
  +gname

Space-separated color stops (hex or names). See also
+gradient for comma-separated stops and preview mode.

EXAMPLES
  +gname gold red
  +gname #ff0000 #00ff00 #0000ff
  +gname reset

SEE ALSO: +gradient, +finger, @moniker
