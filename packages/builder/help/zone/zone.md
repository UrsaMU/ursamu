+ZONE

  Manage zone master objects (ZMOs) and membership.

SYNTAX
  @zone/create <name>
  @zone/destroy <name>
  @zone/add <zone>=<room>
  @zone/remove <zone>=<room>
  @zone/list
  @zone/info <name>

  Exact switches follow the installed zone script.

EXAMPLES
  @zone/create Market
  @zone/add Market=here
  @zone/list

SEE ALSO: +help zone/more, +help staff/batchbuild
