+NAME

  Rename an object. Player names must be unique and valid
  (no spaces or `#=`; max 32). Clears moniker on rename.

SYNTAX
  @name <target>=<new name>

EXAMPLES
  @name me=Galadriel
  @name #5=Shiny Sword

SEE ALSO: +help objects/moniker, +help objects/describe
