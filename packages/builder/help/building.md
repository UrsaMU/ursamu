+BUILDING

  World-building commands: rooms, objects, locks, zones, and
  attribute tools. Most need **builder+**; some need admin+.

TOPICS
  **rooms**     @dig @open @link @unlink
  **objects**   @create @destroy @clone @chown @name @desc
  **attrs**     @set attributes, formats, wipe
  **locks**     @lock and lock expressions
  **actions**   GET/LOOK/MOVE/USE attrs, @oemit
  **zone**      Zone masters and membership
  **staff**     @batchbuild (admin+)

SEE ALSO: +help rooms, +help objects, +help locks
