+JOBS

Staff job lists (Anomaly-style filters).

SYNTAX
  +jobs
  +jobs/all | /mine | /new | /overdue
  +jobs/from <player>
  +jobs/who <player|none>
  +jobs/list <bucket>
  +jobs/pri | /due | /date | /sort
  +jobs/search <pattern>
  +jobs/catchup
  +jobs/silence | /nospam [<#|bucket>]

SEE ALSO: +help job, +help request, +help archive
