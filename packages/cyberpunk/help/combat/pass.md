+PASS

Burn your turn without acting.

Marks your action as taken and advances the tracker. When every
combatant has moved, the round resets and `[acted]` flags clear
automatically — back to the top.

SYNTAX
  +pass

EXAMPLES
  +pass    Eat your action and move on.
  +pass    Use when pinned, unable to act, or playing it cool.

SEE ALSO: +help combat/init, +help combat
