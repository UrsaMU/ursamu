+AMMO

Load specialty ammo into a weapon type.

SYNTAX
  +ammo[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /list              Ammo types and effects.
  /load <wpn>=<type> Load ammo for a weapon type.
  /status            What you have chambered.

EXAMPLES
  +ammo/list
  +ammo/load handgun=armor_piercing

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help combat/effects
