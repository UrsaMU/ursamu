+SUPPRESS

Hose down an area with suppressive fire (FNFF rules).

Requires an autofire-capable weapon. Declares a kill zone.
Roll: WILL + Concentration + 1d10 sets the suppression total.
Anyone in the area rolls `+combat/resist` (REF + Evasion + 1d10)
to see if they catch lead. Failure deals 1d6 damage, bypassing SP.

Chews through 10 rounds from your magazine.

SYNTAX
  +suppress [with <weapon>]

EXAMPLES
  +suppress                         Suppress with your default weapon.
  +suppress with assault_rifle      Suppress with a named weapon.

SEE ALSO: +help combat, +help attack
