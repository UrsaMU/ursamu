+STUN

Non-lethal damage pool. Rubber ammo and choke drain STUN, not HP.
At 0 STUN you are out cold. Short rest restores STUN fully.

SYNTAX
  +sheet/combat
  +sheet

NOTES
  Max STUN = max HP. No wound state or crits on the stun track.

EXAMPLES
  +sheet/combat    View STUN next to HP.
  +rest/start      Recover STUN on a short rest.

SEE ALSO: +help combat, +help rest, +help wound
