+LIFESTYLE

Monthly burn tier. Pay with `/pay` when due.

SYNTAX
  +lifestyle[/switch] [<tier>]

SWITCHES
  /view          Current tier and due date.
  /set <tier>    Change lifestyle tier.
  /pay           Pay this month's burn.

TIERS
  `kibble` `streetrat` `good_prepak` `moderate`
  `corporate` `luxury`

EXAMPLES
  +lifestyle/view           Check tier and due date.
  +lifestyle/set moderate   Switch to moderate.
  +lifestyle/pay            Pay rent.

SEE ALSO: +help eb, +help gig
