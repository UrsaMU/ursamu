+EB

Eurodollar balance and transfers.

SYNTAX
  +eb[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  (none)                  Show your balance.
  /pay <target>=<amount>  Wire eb to someone in the room.
  /give <target>=<amount> (Admin) Grant eb.
  /receive <amount>       (Admin) Add eb to yourself.

EXAMPLES
  +eb                   View balance.
  +eb/pay Rogue=500     Wire 500 eb to Rogue.

SEE ALSO: +help lifestyle, +help market, +help gig
