+DEATHSAVE

Fight to stay on this side of the flatline.

Roll 1d10. Must roll <= (BODY - cumulative penalties) to hold on.
Make this roll at the start of each of your turns while in the
`MORTALLY` wound state. Fail and your state flips to `DEAD`.

Penalties stack from critical injuries with death save modifiers.

SYNTAX
  +deathsave

EXAMPLES
  +deathsave      Roll to survive this turn.
  +deathsave      Roll again next turn if still mortally wounded.

SEE ALSO: +help wound/stabilize, +help wound/crit, +help wound
