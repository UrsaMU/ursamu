+STABILIZE

Pull a mortally wounded edgerunner back from the edge.

Uses TECH + First Aid (DV15) or TECH + Paramedic (DV13). If you
have any Paramedic skill, it kicks in automatically.

Success buys them 1 hour without death saves. You can't
stabilize yourself — find someone who still has hands
(CPR Core rules, p. 227).

SYNTAX
  +stabilize <target>

EXAMPLES
  +stabilize Rogue      Attempt to stabilize Rogue.
  +stabilize Johnny     Attempt to stabilize Johnny.

SEE ALSO: +help wound/deathsave, +help wound/firstaid, +help wound
