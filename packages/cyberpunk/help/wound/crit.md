+CRIT

Roll a critical injury on yourself — Night City takes its pound
of flesh.

Rolls 2d6 on the critical injury table for the given location
and records the result. Use this when an attack against you
rolls 2+ natural 6s on damage dice (CPR Core rules).

The injury and its effects are logged. Some injuries apply a
**Death Save penalty** that stacks with other penalties.

SYNTAX
  +crit [head|body]

EXAMPLES
  +crit          Roll on the body critical injury table.
  +crit head     Roll on the head critical injury table.
  +crit body     Explicitly roll on the body table.

SEE ALSO: +help wound, +help wound/deathsave
