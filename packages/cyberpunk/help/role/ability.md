+ROLE/ABILITY

Activate your role ability. Behavior varies by role.

SYNTAX
  +role/ability [<argument>]

ABILITY NOTES
  **Rockerboy**  COOL+Rank+1d10 vs DV. Crowds: `single` DV 8,
                 `small` DV 10 (default), `large` DV 12.
  **Solo**       Combat Awareness allocation (RP-resolved).
  **Netrunner**  NET actions/turn at rank. Use +netrun.
  **Medtech**    Medicine Specialties. Use +pharma.
  **Tech**       Maker specialty points. Use +craft.
  **Media**      Credibility believability rating.
  **Exec**       Teamwork activation roll vs DV 8.
  **Lawman**     Backup arrival (1d10 <= Rank).
  **Fixer**      Night/Midnight Market access by rank.
  **Nomad**      Family size for support.
EXAMPLES
  +role/ability large    Rockerboy vs large crowd (DV 12).
