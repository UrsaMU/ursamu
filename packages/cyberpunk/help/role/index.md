+ROLE

View your role ability, activate it, or browse rank descriptions.

SYNTAX
  +role[/<switch>] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /info               Role, current rank, and ability description.
                      Default when no switch is given.
  /ability [<size>]   Activate your role ability.
  /rank               All ten rank descriptions for your role.
  /level <1-10>       (Admin) Set your role rank directly.

EXAMPLES
  +role                    Show your role and rank info.
  +role/ability            Activate your role ability.
  +role/rank               Browse all ten rank tiers.
  +role/level 6            (Admin) Set rank to 6.

SEE ALSO: +help role/ability, +help sheet, +help improve
