+FACEDOWN

Reputation contest: COOL + REP + 1d10.
Loser is impressed (~5 min) and should back down.

SYNTAX
  +facedown[/clear] [<target>]

SWITCHES
  (none)   Solo total, or contested vs target.
  /clear   Clear your impressed condition.

EXAMPLES
  +facedown              Show your facedown total.
  +facedown Maelstrom    Contest vs Maelstrom.
  +facedown/clear        Clear impressed.

SEE ALSO: +help rep, +help roll
