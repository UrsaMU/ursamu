+SCAVENGE

Salvage gear and eb. 30-minute cooldown.
Roll INT + Streetwise + Perception + 1d10 vs zone DV.

SYNTAX
  +scavenge [<zone>]

ZONES
  `safe` `contested` `hot` `combat` `hellhole`
  (default: room zone)

EXAMPLES
  +scavenge              Scavenge current zone.
  +scavenge hot          Higher risk, better loot.

SEE ALSO: +help market, +help craft, +help eb
