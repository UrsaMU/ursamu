+CHARGEN/LIFEPATH

Eight backstory tables after role select.

SYNTAX
  +chargen/roll [<stage>] [<n>]
  +chargen/reroll [friends|enemies]
  +chargen/detail <stage> <n>
  +chargen/next

STAGES
  cultural personality motivations family
  friends enemies events role

EXAMPLES
  +chargen/roll              Auto-roll current table.
  +chargen/detail family 3   Read entry 3.
  +chargen/next              Advance when ready.

SEE ALSO: +help chargen, +help chargen/stats
