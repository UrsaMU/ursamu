+CHARGEN/STATS

Allocate STATs and skills (complete method).

SYNTAX
  +chargen/stat <stat>=<value>
  +chargen/skill <skill>=<value>
  +chargen/next

NOTES
  STATs 2-8 in chargen. Skills 0-10. Pool shown on sheet.

EXAMPLES
  +chargen/stat body=6
  +chargen/skill handgun=4
  +chargen/next

SEE ALSO: +help chargen, +help sheet
