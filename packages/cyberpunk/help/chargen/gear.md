+CHARGEN/GEAR

Lifestyle, starting chrome, and loadout.

SYNTAX
  +chargen/lifestyle <tier>
  +chargen/chrome <list|name|remove <name>>
  +chargen/gear <list|name|remove <name>>
  +chargen/next

EXAMPLES
  +chargen/lifestyle streetrat
  +chargen/chrome neural link
  +chargen/gear medium pistol
  +chargen/next

SEE ALSO: +help chargen, +help chargen/done
