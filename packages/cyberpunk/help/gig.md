+GIG

IC Datahaven **gig board** (not staff +jobs).

SYNTAX
  +gig[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /list              Browse open gigs.
  /view <id>         Full posting details.
  /post [<n>]        (Fixer/Admin) Post from templates.
  /take <id>         Accept a gig.
  /complete <id>     Claim payout (+1 rep).
  /abandon <id>      Return gig to the board.
  /payout <t> <amt>  (Admin) Custom payout.

EXAMPLES
  +gig/list              Browse the board.
  +gig/take abc123       Accept a contract.
  +gig/complete abc123   Collect eddies.

SEE ALSO: +help eb, +help rep
