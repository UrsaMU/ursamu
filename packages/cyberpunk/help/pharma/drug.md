+DRUG

Use drugs from inventory. One dose of each at a time.

SYNTAX
  +drug[/switch] [<name>]

SWITCHES
  /list       Catalog and effects.
  /active     Running effects and timers.
  /use <name> Take a dose you own.
  /purge      Rapidetox -- clear all drugs.

EXAMPLES
  +drug/use speedheal
  +drug/active
  +drug/purge

SEE ALSO: +help pharma/stim, +help heal
