+MARKET

Buy, sell, and haggle on the Fixer gray market.

SYNTAX
  +market[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /open [midnight]  Open market (Fixer rank 5 / 9).
  /close            Shut your market.
  /browse           Listings in this room.
  /sell <i>=<p>=<c> Post an item.
  /buy <id>         Purchase a listing.
  /haggle <id>      (Fixer) Knock price down.
  /list             Your listings everywhere.

EXAMPLES
  +market/browse                  See local stock.
  +market/sell Pistol=500=costly  List a pistol.
  +market/buy abc123              Buy listing abc123.
