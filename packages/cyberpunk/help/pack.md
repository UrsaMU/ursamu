+PACK

Call your **Nomad** pack for support. 24h cooldown per type.

SYNTAX
  +pack[/switch] [<type>]

SWITCHES
  /status           Available support and cooldowns.
  /request <type>   Call in support.

TYPES
  supplies  Rank 1+   ammo and basics
  backup    Rank 4+   armed pack members
  medical   Rank 4+   heal 2d6 HP
  haven     Rank 7+   safe house

EXAMPLES
  +pack/status              Check pack support.
  +pack/request medical     Call a pack medic.

SEE ALSO: +help improve, +help rep
