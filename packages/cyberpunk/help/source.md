+SOURCE

**Fixer** Operator: source private listings (1h cooldown).
COOL + Streetwise + black_market_contacts + 1d10 vs tier DV.

SYNTAX
  +source <item>=<category>
  +source/list
  +source/buy <id>

SWITCHES
  /list   Your active sourced listings (max 3, 24h).
  /buy    Purchase a listing (full market price).

EXAMPLES
  +source Trauma Rippers=costly   Source a costly item.
  +source/list                    View listings.
  +source/buy a1b2c3d4            Buy by id.

SEE ALSO: +help market, +help role
