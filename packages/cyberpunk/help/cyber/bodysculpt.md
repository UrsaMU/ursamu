+BODYSCULPT

Cosmetic or exotic body mods. Costs HL.

SYNTAX
  +bodysculpt[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /list            Browse modifications.
  /do <mod>        Perform a mod on yourself.
  /view            Your completed sculpt work.

EXAMPLES
  +bodysculpt/list
  +bodysculpt/do light_tattoo

SEE ALSO: +help cyber, +help humanity
