+ARMOR

View, equip, remove, and repair **armor**. Body and head slots.
SP absorbs damage and ablates on hit.

SYNTAX
  +armor[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /list           Browse the armor catalog.
  /view           Show worn armor and current SP.
  /wear <name>    Equip armor (`light_armorjack`).
  /remove <slot>  Strip `body` or `head` armor.
  /repair <slot>  Patch SP (100 eb/SP, TECH roll).

EXAMPLES
  +armor/wear leather_jacket   Equip body armor.
  +armor/repair head           Patch head armor.

SEE ALSO: +help combat, +help repair
