+CRAFT

**Tech** fabrication, blueprints, and field repair.

SYNTAX
  +craft[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /list                    Active projects.
  /makerpoints             Maker specialty points.
  /start <item>=<cat>      Begin craft (pays materials).
  /check <id>              Roll to finish a ready project.
  /cancel <id>             Abandon (materials lost).
  /blueprint <item>=<cat>  Log a blueprint.
  /fieldrepair <item> <DV> Quick field repair roll.

EXAMPLES
  +craft/start Pistol=costly   Begin a costly pistol.
  +craft/check abc123          Complete project abc123.
