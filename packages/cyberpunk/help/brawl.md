+BRAWL

Unarmed FNFF moves. DEX + Brawling unless noted.
**Pin** and **choke** need a grab first.

SYNTAX
  +brawl/<move> <target>

SWITCHES
  /grab    vs DEX+Evasion. Target cannot move freely.
  /throw   vs DV15. BODY as HP; target prone.
  /disarm  vs DEX+weapon skill. Knocks weapon free.
  /pin     Needs grab. Immobilize target.
  /choke   Needs grab. BODY HP/round; 3 hits drop them.

EXAMPLES
  +brawl/grab Rogue       Grab Rogue.
  +brawl/choke Rogue      Choke a grabbed target.

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help wound
