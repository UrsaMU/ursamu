+INCOME

Role-based **passive income**. Collect when the timer is ready.

SYNTAX
  +income[/switch]

SWITCHES
  /status    Show source, amount, and history (default).
  /collect   Collect available payout.

EXAMPLES
  +income            Check income status.
  +income/collect    Collect your payout.

SEE ALSO: +help eb, +help role, +help lifestyle
