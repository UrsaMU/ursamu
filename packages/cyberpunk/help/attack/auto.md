+ATTACK/AUTO

Autofire. DV by weapon class and range (errata p.173).
Damage 2d6 x margin, capped by autofireMax. Burns 10 rounds.

SYNTAX
  +attack/auto <target> with <weapon> [at <range>]

RANGES
  close medium long vlong extreme

EXAMPLES
  +attack/auto Rogue with smg
  +attack/auto Rogue with assault_rifle at long

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help combat/suppress
