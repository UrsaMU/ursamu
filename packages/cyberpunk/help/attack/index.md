+ATTACK

Attack a target in the room. Armor ablates on hit.

SYNTAX
  +attack[/<mode>] <target> [with <weapon>]

SWITCHES
  /aimed   -8 to hit, +1 damage. Single/semi only.
  /auto    Autofire burst (see +help attack/auto).
  /melee   DEX + Melee Weapon.
  /called  Called shot (see +help attack/called).

EXAMPLES
  +attack Rogue                     Default weapon.
  +attack/aimed Rogue               Aimed shot.
  +attack/melee Rogue with knife    Melee strike.

SEE ALSO: +help attack/called, +help attack/auto
