+ATTACK/CALLED

Called shot: **-8** to hit. Location effects last the scene.

SYNTAX
  +attack/called <target>/<location> [with <weapon>]

LOCATIONS
  head   x2 damage after armor
  arm    BODY DV15 or drop held item
  leg    MOVE halved
  hand   Hand unusable
  eye    -3 Awareness/Perception

EXAMPLES
  +attack/called Rogue/head with rifle
  +attack/called Rogue/arm

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help brawl
