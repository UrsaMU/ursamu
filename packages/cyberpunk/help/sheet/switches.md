+SHEET SWITCHES

Extra sheet views beyond the default full sheet.

SYNTAX
  +sheet/<switch> [<target>]

SWITCHES
  /skills    Skill block only.
  /cyber     Installed cyberware.
  /gear      Inventory and loadout.
  /combat    HP, armor, wounds, luck.

EXAMPLES
  +sheet/skills
  +sheet/cyber Rogue

SEE ALSO: +help sheet, +help score
