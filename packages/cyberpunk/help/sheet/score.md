+SCORE

Quick combat vitals strip — HP, armor, wound, stun, SW, death.

SYNTAX
  +score [<player>]

NOTES
  Fight readout: HP bar, stun, body/head SP, wound state, SW
  threshold, death save, EMP, HL, luck pool, eddies.
  Web /play shows the same panel as the chargen vitals rail.

EXAMPLES
  +score           Your combat strip.
  +score Rogue     Rogue's strip.

SEE ALSO: +help sheet, +help sheet/combat
