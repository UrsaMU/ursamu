+SHEET

Display your character sheet, or another edgerunner's sheet.

SYNTAX
  +sheet[/<switch>] [<player>]

SWITCHES
  /stats     Stats panel only.
  /skills    Skills panel — all skills with a rank above 0.
  /cyber     Chrome and humanity (cyberware list, EMP, HL lost).
  /combat    HP bar, wound state, armor, critical injuries,
             active drug effects.
  /economy   Eddies, lifestyle tier, reputation deeds.

EXAMPLES
  +sheet               View your own full sheet.
  +sheet Rogue         View Rogue's sheet.
  +sheet/combat        Your HP, wound state, and injuries.
  +sheet/cyber         Your chrome and current humanity.

SEE ALSO: +help sheet/switches, +help sheet/score, +help role
