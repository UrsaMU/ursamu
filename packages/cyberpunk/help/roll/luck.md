+LUCK

Spend Luck points to boost a roll after it is made.

Each point spent adds +1 directly to your last roll total.
Your Luck pool resets at the start of each run. With no
argument, displays your current pool.

SYNTAX
  +luck [<points>]

EXAMPLES
  +luck        Show your current Luck pool and maximum.
  +luck 1      Spend 1 Luck point (+1 to your last roll).
  +luck 3      Spend 3 Luck points (+3 to your last roll).

SEE ALSO: +help roll
