+REP

Street Cred (0-10). Affects facedowns and Fixer access.

SYNTAX
  +rep[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /view          Score, tier, and deeds.
  /deed <text>   Record a deed (last 10 kept).
  /add <amount>  (Admin) Adjust rep.

EXAMPLES
  +rep                         View street cred.
  +rep/deed Took down MaxTac   Record a deed.
  +rep/add 2                   (Admin) +2 rep.

SEE ALSO: +help facedown, +help improve/ip
