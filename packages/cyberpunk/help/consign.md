+CONSIGN

Submit gear to a **Fixer** stall for sale (85% on sell).

SYNTAX
  +consign[/switch] [<argument>]

SWITCHES
  /submit <item>=<price>=<cat>  Hand item to open stall.
  /list                         Your consignments.
  /pending                      (Fixer) Pending requests.
  /approve <id>                 (Fixer) List item for sale.
  /decline <id>                 (Fixer) Return item to owner.

EXAMPLES
  +consign/submit Pistol=800=costly
  +consign/approve abc123
