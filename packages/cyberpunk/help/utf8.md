+UTF8

Toggle UTF-8 glyphs for your own UI output.
Room broadcasts stay Latin-1 for mixed clients.

SYNTAX
  +utf8
  +utf8 on
  +utf8 off

EXAMPLES
  +utf8 on     Enable Unicode frames and gauges.
  +utf8 off    Revert to ASCII-safe output.
  +sheet       Preview with current glyph mode.

SEE ALSO: +help sheet
