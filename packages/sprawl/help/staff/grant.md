+STAFF/GRANT

See also: +help staff (index)

Free **b¥**, catalog **gear**, and **AP** for
players with a sprawl sheet.

SYNTAX
  +staff/cash <player>=<n>
  +staff/gear <player>=<slug>
  +staff/ap <player>=<n>

NOTES
  cash n may be negative (floor 0).
  gear = market/catalog slug (+market).
  AP raises pool and lifetime (level).

EXAMPLES
  +staff/cash Neon=1000
  +staff/gear Neon=pkd-45
  +staff/ap Neon=50
SEE ALSO: +help staff/advance, +help market
