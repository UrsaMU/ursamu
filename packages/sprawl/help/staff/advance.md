+STAFF/ADVANCE

See also: +help staff (index)

**AP** funds picks. Players spend with
`+advance/<track>` (100 AP). Level = life/100.

SYNTAX
  +advance/ready <player>     +25 AP mission
  +advance/session [player]   +10 AP session
  +staff/ap <player>=<n>      any amount

TRACKS
  morphology equilibrium reaction cognition
  affinity resilience loadout edge

EXAMPLES
  +advance/ready Alice
  +advance/session Bob
  +staff/ap Alice=100

SEE ALSO: +help advance, +help staff/grant
