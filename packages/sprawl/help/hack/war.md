+HACK/WAR

See also: +help hack (overview)

SYNTAX
  +console/war <player>
  +console/run <soft>=<player>
  +paradox/defend <attackTotal>

RULES
  Pool vs their Firewall. Breach → wipe +
  AP ping. Nimbus immune-ap shrugs AP soft.

EXAMPLES
  +console/war Alice
  +console/run quiver=Alice
  +console/run brain-drain=Bob
  +paradox/defend 16
