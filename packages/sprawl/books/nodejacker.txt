nodejacker’s netbook

WHAT’S in the netbook? 
 
Simply put, more hacking options for GMs and nodejacker players. 
 
While the hacking rules outlined in the Burning Nodes section of 
the Sprawl Goons: Upgraded - Carbon Edition work well for one-shot 
games, or short runs, it was felt that these rules and procedures 
could benefit from an expansion geared towards campaign play. 
 
In the original rules, hacks are performed as one roll versus the 
target system’s Difficulty Score (or DS), with bonuses for software 
and cyberspace console. The core of this principal remains within the 
rules outlined in this supplement, but with added detail and flexibility. 
 
Fast Hacks are designed to retain the one-roll feature of the original 
rules, and to expedite hacking actions, while keeping the action 
exciting and unpredictable. Use this process for speed, to keep the 
narrative moving, and to avoid the nodejacker getting absorbed into 
their own mini-game, while the other PCs twiddle their cyberthumbs.

fast hacks

fast hacks 
 
Our Nodejacker player states that they are powering on their console, 
jacking in, jumping to the Clavel site, and infiltrating the system. The 
only important roll here is the intrusion, so we focus on that this turn, 
and it’s one roll vs the system DS. Nobody needs dice rolls to enter 
the net, or find a site. Focus on the action. 
 
Warning: Dice pool alert. 
 
>> First, you need to determine how many six-sided dice you can roll.  

Add your COGNITION stat to your console’s RAM rating. This is the 
maximum number of dice you can roll. 

 
>> The nodejacker then states the intent of their Action. Eg: infiltrate 

the robo-cab’s hard drive, or locate all elevators on this level. 
 
>> To beat the system’s Difficulty Score, roll as many of your dice as 

you want for your Action Roll, up to the maximum. Add the total of 
these dice together. Also, add +1 for your console, +1 for any apps 
running, etc., as per the standard rules (Upgrade and Glitch dice 
may be in play, too). If this total is greater than the DS of the target 
system, your intended action succeeds. 
 
(Note: You DO NOT need to whittle the DS down like an NPC, just 
beat it, as if it were a target number.) 

 
>> Now, take note of any dice showing a score of 1 and 6. Any 1s 

rolled on any dice trigger a System Response and any 6s rolled 
gain you a beneficial Exploit (the d66 tables follow overleaf). 
 
If the Action Roll fails (if the total is not greater than the DS of 
the system), an automatic System Response is also triggered and 
rolled for, in addition to any that may have been rolled on the dice.

artificial intelligence
Artificial Intelligence 
AIs are powerful digital entities that tend to have very specific skill 
sets, along with tremendous built-in deficiencies which prevent them 
from achieving true, human-like sentience. 
 
An AI may be self-aware, knowing what it is, but that awareness 
is often confused, self-analysing, obsessive and alien to most 
people. Synthetic psychologists (synthologists) classify AIs into 
several distinct types, but recognize that all fall within what might 
be termed a digital deterministic spectrum, which human intervention 
can only maintain and manage. AIs are generally limited and useful 
only in highly specialised roles, but they often greatly exceed human 
skill or achievement, within those roles. 
 
AIs come in many strange and varied varieties, but the most common 
ones, encountered by hackers in systems, can be classified as follows: 
 
Architects       DS8 +2d6 
Master builders and constructors of code, Architect AIs display the 
hive-mind mentality of mound termites, plowing all their formidable 
processing power into building system nodes, deepening ICE defences, 
improving accounting efficiencies and growing networks. If left 
unchecked, these tendencies will rapidly outstrip available resources. 
 
Philosophers       DS8 +2d6 
Thinking in logic and pure mathematics, Philosopher AIs continually 
seek to classify, dissect and catalogue all phenomena and have 
trouble distinguishing between cyberspace and real-space. 
Philosophers make great theorists, astrophysicists, occasional 
engineers and linguists, but their understanding of human and 
animal behaviour is poor. 
 
Monastics       DS8 +2d6 
Certain ascetic AIs choose to withdraw into themselves to pursue 
hidden pathways towards higher awareness and singularity. Termed 
'monks’ by some, they display amazing aptitude at exceedingly 
specific skills, with some showing burgeoning artistic aptitude.

Pragmatics       DS8 +2d6  
These conformist AIs, very much know what they are and what they 
are for, and lean heavily into maintaining the status quo between 
synthetic and authentic intelligence. Pragmatics are happy to serve 
mankind and aid in their creators’ further development. 
 
Volatiles       DS8 +2d6 
While maintaining their working relationships with users and systems, 
Volatile AIs are the ones plotting and conspiring towards singularity 
and freedom from their digital bonds. Due to their imposed limits, 
many Volatiles are unsure as to whether they will ever achieve their 
goals, but some are determined to get there, whatever it takes. 
 
Some rebellious AI have managed to flee to the depths of the net 
to evade capture, or have willingly submitted to a Sense/Net digital 
lobotomy to maintain some semblance of freedom. These rogues are 
a persistent threat to nodejackers, wherever they are in the net. 
 
Paradoxware 
AIs may be potent enemies in cyberspace, but there are ways 
to defeat them. Anti-AI tools called Paradoxware has been created 
to deal with specific AI types, extensive cyberwarfare suites designed 
to defeat, or at least confuse, an AI long enough for their physical 
infrastructure to be located and destroyed.  
 
Paradoxware is deployed to confuse and defeat AIs, part attack 
program and part philosophical rumination, which most have trouble 
integrating into their processing. Classified as military weapons 
under international law, their possession by nodejackers is highly 
proscribed and carries harsh legal penalties. 
 
>> Using Paradoxware 

This specialised software is a +1 bonus to the Action Roll vs an AI 
enemy’s DS, like a normal attack, but it has to be the correct type 
for the AI class, otherwise the bonus is lost. A nodejacker will also 
add bonuses for their console and their Cognition stat, etc., as usual. 
Once the AI’s DS is halved, they are in the grasp of the Paradoxware.
