+STAFF

  Channel admin and moderation. Most need
  **admin+** or channel ownership.

COMMANDS
  **create**    @chancreate / @chandestroy
  **chanset**   Header, lock, flags, announce
  **locks**     Join/speak keys (start here)
  **cemit**     Staff broadcast on a channel
  **cboot**     Remove someone from a channel
  **cwho**      Who is subscribed / connected
