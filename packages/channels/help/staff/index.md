+STAFF

  Channel admin and moderation. Most need **admin+** or channel
  ownership.

COMMANDS
  **create**    @chancreate / @chandestroy
  **chanset**   Header, locks, flags, announce, history.
  **locks**     Join/speak lock expressions.
  **cemit**     Staff broadcast on a channel.
  **cboot**     Remove someone from a channel.
  **cwho**      Who is subscribed / connected.
