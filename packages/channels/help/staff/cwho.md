+CWHO

  List who is on a channel (owner or admin+).

SYNTAX
  @cwho <channel>
  @cwho/all <channel>

SWITCHES
  /all  Include offline subscribers, not only connected.

EXAMPLES
  @cwho Public
  @cwho/all Staff

SEE ALSO: +help staff/cboot, +help staff
