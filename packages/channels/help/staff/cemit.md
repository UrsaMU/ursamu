+CEMIT

  Broadcast on a channel without using a personal alias
  (owner or admin+).

SYNTAX
  @cemit <channel>=<message>
  @cemit/noheader <channel>=<message>

SWITCHES
  /noheader  Omit the channel header prefix.

EXAMPLES
  @cemit Public=Restart in five minutes.
  @cemit/noheader Staff=Alert.

SEE ALSO: +help staff/cboot, +help staff
