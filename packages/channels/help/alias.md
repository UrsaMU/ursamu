+ALIAS

  Use a channel alias to speak, pose, or check who is listening.

SYNTAX
  <alias> <message>
  <alias> :<pose>
  <alias> ;<pose>
  <alias> on
  <alias> off
  <alias> who

SWITCHES
  on    Turn the channel alias on (receive messages).
  off   Turn the channel alias off (mute the channel).
  who   List connected players currently on the channel.

EXAMPLES
  pub Hello, World!
  pub :waves to everyone.
  pub off
  pub who
