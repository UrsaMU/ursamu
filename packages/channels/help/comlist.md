+COMLIST

  List all of your current channel aliases and their status.

SYNTAX
  comlist

  Displays each alias, the channel it maps to, any comtitle set,
  and whether the alias is currently on or off.

EXAMPLES
  comlist

  Output:
    pub -> Public [on]
    st  -> Staff  [off] <GM>

SEE ALSO: +help addcom, +help delcom, +help allcom, +help comtitle
