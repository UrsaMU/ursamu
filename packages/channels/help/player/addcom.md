+ADDCOM

  Join a channel by binding a short alias to it.

SYNTAX
  addcom <alias>=<channel>

  Alias and channel name are case-sensitive. You may hold more
  than one alias for the same channel.

EXAMPLES
  addcom pub=Public
  addcom st=Staff

SEE ALSO: +help player/delcom, +help player/alias
