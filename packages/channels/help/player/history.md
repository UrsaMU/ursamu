+HISTORY

  Read recent channel traffic. Logging must be on for that
  channel (`@chanset <chan>/log=on`).

SYNTAX
  @chanhistory <channel>[=<lines>]
  @chantranscript <channel>=<lines>

  History shows recent lines. Transcript dumps plain text to you.

EXAMPLES
  @chanhistory Public
  @chanhistory Public=50
  @chantranscript Public=100

SEE ALSO: +help staff/chanset, +help player
