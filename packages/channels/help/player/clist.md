+CLIST

  List public channels and owners. Hidden channels omit
  unless you are staff. Aliases: @clist, @channel.

SYNTAX
  clist
  clist/full
  clist/headers

SWITCHES
  /full     Flags (H/M/L/A), owner, users online.
  /headers  Channel header prefixes.

EXAMPLES
  clist
  clist/full

SEE ALSO: +help player/addcom, +help channels
