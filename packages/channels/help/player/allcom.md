+ALLCOM

  Act on every channel alias at once.

SYNTAX
  allcom on
  allcom off
  allcom who

SWITCHES
  on    Receive all subscribed channels.
  off   Mute all subscribed channels.
  who   Who is connected on each channel.

EXAMPLES
  allcom on
  allcom off
  allcom who

SEE ALSO: +help player/alias, +help player/comlist
