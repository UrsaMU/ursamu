+PLAYER

  Player channel commands. Join with an alias, then use that
  alias to talk. Leading `@` is optional on most commands.

COMMANDS
  **addcom**     Join a channel with an alias.
  **delcom**     Drop one alias.
  **clearcom**   Drop every alias.
  **comlist**    List your aliases and on/off status.
  **allcom**     On/off/who for all aliases at once.
  **comtitle**   Title shown before your name.
  **clist**      List public channels.
  **alias**      How to speak with an alias.
  **history**    Recent messages and transcripts.

SEE ALSO: +help channels, +help staff
