+COMTITLE

  Set a personal title prefix displayed before your name on a channel.

SYNTAX
  comtitle <alias>=<title>
  comtitle <alias>=

  The title is prepended to your display name whenever you speak on
  that channel. Clear it by leaving the value empty.

  Whether the title replaces your name or is appended to it depends
  on the channel's spoof/nospoof setting.

EXAMPLES
  comtitle pub=Lord
  comtitle staff=GM
  comtitle pub=

SEE ALSO: +help addcom, +help alias
