+CLIST

  List public channels and their owners.

SYNTAX
  @clist
  @clist/full
  @clist/headers

SWITCHES
  /full     Detailed stats including flags, owner, and user count.
  /headers  Lists channels with their custom header prefixes.

EXAMPLES
  @clist
  @clist/full
  @clist/headers

SEE ALSO: +help channels, +help addcom
