+CEMIT

  Emit a message to a channel (channel owner or staff only).

SYNTAX
  @cemit[/noheader] <channel>=<message>

SWITCHES
  /noheader  Sends the message without the channel name prefix.

EXAMPLES
  @cemit Public=The game will restart soon.
  @cemit/noheader Staff=Alert.

SEE ALSO: +help channels
