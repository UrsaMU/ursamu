+CHANSTAFF — Channel Staff Commands

  Commands reserved for channel owners and admin-level staff.

CHANNEL MANAGEMENT (admin+)
  **@chancreate** <name>[=<header>]  Create a new channel.
  **@chandestroy** <name>            Permanently destroy a channel.
  **@chanset** <name>/<prop>=<val>   Modify channel properties.

MODERATION (channel owner or admin+)
  **@cemit** <channel>=<message>     Broadcast without speaking.
  **@cboot** <channel>=<object>      Remove a player from channel.
  **@cwho** <channel>                List all subscribers.

SEE ALSO: +help chancreate, +help chanset, +help chanlock,
          +help cemit, +help cboot, +help cwho
