+ALLCOM

  Perform an action on every channel alias at once.

SYNTAX
  allcom on
  allcom off
  allcom who

SWITCHES
  on    Turn every alias on (receive all channels).
  off   Turn every alias off (mute all channels).
  who   List connected players on every subscribed channel.

EXAMPLES
  allcom on
  allcom off
  allcom who

SEE ALSO: +help alias, +help addcom, +help delcom, +help comlist
