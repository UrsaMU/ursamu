FEED -- Drink from vessels or Herd

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Scene feeding. You regain Blood; mortals take lethal.

SYNTAX
  +feed                     Show Blood and Herd.
  +feed <target> [=n]       Feed on a vessel (default 1).
  +feed/herd [=n]           Feed from Herd background.

NOTES
  Kindred vessels lose BP first. Bond may deepen (+bond).
  Ventrue: feedingPreference. Giovanni: 1 BP/turn.
  Prey Exclusion can block a vessel class.

EXAMPLES
  +feed Alice=2
  +feed/herd=3
