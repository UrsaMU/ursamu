RITUALS

Blood-magic rituals for Thaumaturgy and Necromancy (V20 p.230,
p.177). Rituals are learned individually; each requires a school
rating (Thaumaturgy or Necromancy dots) equal to its level to learn
or cast. Casting is Intelligence + Occult vs difficulty 3 + level.

SYNTAX
  +ritual                   List rituals you know.
  +ritual/list [school]     Browse the full catalog
                            (thaumaturgy | necromancy).
  +ritual/book <name>       Detail card: level, cost, components.
  +ritual/learn <name>      Learn a ritual (level <= your rating).
  +ritual/forget <name>     Drop a ritual.
  +ritual/cast <name>       Cast it (blood cost varies by ritual).
  +ritual/teach <t>/<name>  Grant a ritual directly (staff).

EXAMPLES
  +ritual/learn pavis of foul presence
  +ritual/cast wake with evening's freshness
  +ritual/list necromancy

NOTE
  You cannot cast rituals while in frenzy, Rotschreck, torpor, or
  staked. Botched castings are at Storyteller discretion.
