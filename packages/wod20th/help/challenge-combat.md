CHALLENGE -- COMBAT TYPES

See also: +help challenge (overview)

Three combat-typed challenges resolve by first-blood:

  single-combat   Tooth and fist; no fetish weapons.
  klaive-duel     Both Garou must wield a fetish weapon.
  death-duel      The gravest writ. Requires sept-leader consent.

After +challenge/resolve, the duel is armed: the next landed +attack
between the two participants resolves the challenge and awards Glory
to the striker. Death-duels run to incapacitation; lighter combat
challenges end on first blood.

SEPT CONSENT (death-duel)
  A sept leader -- either the named sept leader or the alpha of any
  pack belonging to the sept -- must run +challenge/consent <id>.
  Staff may consent on the sept's behalf.
