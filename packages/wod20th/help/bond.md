BOND -- Blood bonds (1-3 drinks)

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Each drink of Kindred vitae deepens the bond toward that
  regnant (max 3 = full thrall).

SYNTAX
  +bond                     List your bonds.
  +bond/drink <regnant>     One drink toward the bond.
  +bond/break <thrall> [id] (Staff) Weaken or clear bond.

NOTES
  Unbondable merit blocks all blood bonds.

EXAMPLES
  +bond/drink Prince
  +bond
  +bond/break Alice

SEE ALSO: +help feed, +help embrace, +help ghoul, +help vtm
