DERANGE -- Permanent derangements

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Lasting mental scars. Malkavians must keep at least one.

SYNTAX
  +derange                    Your derangements.
  +derange/list               Catalog of known derangements.
  +derange/add <name>         Add to yourself.
  +derange/add <name> <tgt>   (Staff) Add on a target.
  +derange/remove <name>      Remove (Malk keeps >=1).
  +derange/remove <n> <tgt>   (Staff) Remove on a target.

EXAMPLES
  +derange/list
  +derange/add paranoia
  +derange/remove obsession

SEE ALSO: +help humanity, +help beast, +help vtm
