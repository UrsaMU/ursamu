COTERIE -- Kindred social unit

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Small band of Kindred (pack analogue). Leader invites and
  may disband.

SYNTAX
  +coterie                  Show coterie or invites.
  +coterie/list | /who <n>  List or show a coterie.
  +coterie/create <name>    Form a coterie; you lead.
  +coterie/invite <player>  (Leader) Invite a Kindred.
  +coterie/accept|decline   Resolve a pending invite.
  +coterie/leave | /disband Leave, or leader disbands.
  +coterie/purpose <text>   (Leader) Set purpose.

EXAMPLES
  +coterie/create Night Howlers
  +coterie/invite Rook
  +coterie/accept
