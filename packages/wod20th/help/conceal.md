CONCEAL / REVEAL

Tuck a worn or wielded item out of sight, or produce it again.

SYNTAX
  +conceal <item>
  +reveal <item>

DETAILS
  Concealment only matters for gear you are wearing or wielding;
  loose items in your pockets are already out of view. Items with
  concealability "N" (Not concealable) cannot be hidden.

  Concealing is silent -- the room sees no pose. Revealing poses
  the item back into view so others can react.

EXAMPLES
  +conceal pistol    Tuck the pistol out of sight (silent).
  +reveal pistol     Produce the pistol; the room sees it.

SEE ALSO: +help spot, +help wod20th
