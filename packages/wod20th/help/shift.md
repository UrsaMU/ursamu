SHIFT -- Werewolf Form Shifting

See also: +help wod20th (overview)

Garou move between five forms. Each applies static modifiers to Strength,
Dexterity, Stamina, Manipulation, and Appearance.

SYNTAX
  +shift              Show current form and the modifier table.
  +shift <form>       Shift to <form>.
  +shift/who          (Staff) List nearby characters and their forms.

FORMS
  Homid    Human form. Natural for homid breed.
  Glabro   Near-man. Larger, stronger, hairier.
  Crinos   Wolf-man war form. Default for metis.
  Hispo    Dire-wolf. Massive prehistoric wolf.
  Lupus    Wolf form. Natural for lupus breed.

  While frenzied you are locked into Crinos and cannot shift out
  until the frenzy ends (see +help frenzy).

EXAMPLES
  +shift              Show the table.
  +shift crinos       Shift to war-form.
  +shift homid        Return to human form.
