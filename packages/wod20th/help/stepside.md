STEP SIDEWAYS

Cross the Gauntlet between the material world and the Umbra.

  Stare into a reflective surface and roll your Gnosis pool against the
  local Gauntlet rating (default 6). Rooms may override this -- /where
  reports the current Gauntlet value. Each success speeds the shift; one
  success is enough to cross. Failure or botch costs 1 temporary Gnosis.
  Wound penalties reduce the Gnosis pool used for the roll and are
  annotated in the result as "(wound -N)".

SYNTAX
  +stepside                Attempt to shift across the Gauntlet.
  +stepside/check          Roll without committing -- preview the outcome.
  +stepside/where          Show your current location.
  +stepside/moonbridge <caern>  Open a moon bridge to another caern.

SWITCHES
  /check       Preview a roll. No persistence, no Gnosis cost.
  /where       Report Umbral or material status and local Gauntlet.
  /moonbridge  Open a 1-Gnosis bridge between caerns (must stand in one).

NOTES
  Successfully crossing flips your reality flag between the material
  plane and the Penumbra; descriptions tagged for the Umbra (see
  @desc/penumbra) are shown to viewers on that side.

EXAMPLES
  +stepside           Try to step sideways.
  +stepside/check     What would happen if I tried right now?
  +stepside/where     Am I in the spirit world?
