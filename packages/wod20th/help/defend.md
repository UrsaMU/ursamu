DEFEND -- Declare a reactive defense

  Per W20 (p. 288): defenses must be DECLARED before the attacker
  rolls, and they cost an action. Undeclared = unopposed.

SYNTAX
  +defend/dodge        Dex + Athletics. Always legal.
  +defend/block        Dex + Brawl. Only vs Brawl/Melee attacks.
  +defend/parry        Dex + Melee. Only vs Brawl/Melee; need a wielded
                       melee weapon.
  +defend/clear        Cancel a declared defense.
  +defend              Show your current declared defense.

NOTES
  Declared defense rolls full pool minus any +split penalty.
  Consumed by the next legal attack against you, even if it misses.
  Illegal defenses (e.g. /block vs firearms) stay queued -- the
  declaration is skipped for that attack but not cleared.

  +defend with no +split is "abort to defense": full pool, your turn's
  action is spent. +split 2 + +defend lets you defend AND act, each
  at -1.

EXAMPLES
  +defend/dodge        Ready a Dex+Athletics dodge.
  +defend/parry        Parry a sword swing with your klaive.
  +defend              See what's queued.
