DOMAIN -- City claims and offices

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Domains hold a ruler and offices (Prince, Sheriff, etc.).

SYNTAX
  +domain                   List domains.
  +domain/who <name>        Show a domain.
  +domain/create <name>     (Staff) Create.
  +domain/sect|area|ruler <n>=<v>
                            (Staff) Set fields.
  +domain/office <d>/<w>=<o>
                            (Staff) Appoint office.
  +domain/unoffice <d>/<w>  (Staff) Clear office.
  +domain/delete <name>     (Staff) Delete.

EXAMPLES
  +domain/create Neon District
  +domain/office Neon District/Cass=sheriff
