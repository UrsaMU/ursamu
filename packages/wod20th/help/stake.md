STAKE -- Heart-stake paralysis

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  A stake through the heart freezes a Kindred until pulled.
  Staked characters cannot act.

SYNTAX
  +stake                      Your stake status.
  +stake <target>             (Staff) Apply stake.
  +stake/pull <target>        (Staff) Remove stake.

EXAMPLES
  +stake
  +stake Alice
  +stake/pull Alice

SEE ALSO: +help torpor, +help attack, +help beast, +help vtm
