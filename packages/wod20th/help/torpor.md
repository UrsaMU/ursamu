TORPOR -- Kindred death-sleep

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  When a vampire hits Incapacitated with lethal or aggravated
  damage, they enter torpor. Most actions stay blocked until
  staff (or story) raises them.

SYNTAX
  +torpor                     Your torpor state.
  +torpor/rise <target>       (Staff) Clear torpor and wounds.

EXAMPLES
  +torpor
  +torpor/rise Alice

SEE ALSO: +help blood, +help beast, +help stake, +help vtm
