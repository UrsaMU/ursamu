WEAR -- Don and remove armor

  +wear and +remove control whether armor or a shield you are carrying
  is worn. Only items with kind=armor or kind=shield may be worn.

SYNTAX
  +wear <item>
  +remove <item>

EXAMPLES
  +wear leather jacket
  +remove leather jacket
  +wear riot shield

NOTES
  Worn armor contributes its armorRating to your soak.
