MOOT -- Sept gatherings

A moot is the canonical Garou gathering held at a caern. It runs through
four phases: Opening Howl, Inner Sky, Cracking the Bone, and the Revel.
Closing the moot awards every attendee +0.5 temp Honor (showing up
matters). See +help moot-phases for the canon detail.

SYNTAX
  +moot                              List currently open moots.
  +moot/list                         Same as no switch.
  +moot/info                         Local moot status (in caern room).
  +moot/schedule <sept>=<when>       (Staff or Master of the Howl)
  +moot/open                         (Staff or MotH, in caern room)
  +moot/attend                       Join the moot.
  +moot/phase <next|<name>|close>    (Staff or MotH) Advance phase.
  +moot/award <plr>=<track>:<N> <r>  (Staff, during Bone/Revel)
  +moot/close                        (Staff or MotH) End the moot.

EXAMPLES
  +moot/schedule Sept of the Pines=Saturday at moonrise
  +moot/open                         Begins the Opening Howl.
  +moot/attend                       Garou claim a place at the moot.
  +moot/phase next                   Walks the phases in canon order.
  +moot/award Cassidy=honor:2 spoke truth

SEE ALSO: +help moot-phases, +help sept, +help caern
