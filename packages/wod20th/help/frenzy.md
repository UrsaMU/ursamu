FRENZY -- Werewolf rage and berserker rules

  Garou are creatures of Rage. Certain triggers force a Willpower test
  to retain control; failure means berserk (Crinos) or fox (flight)
  frenzy lasting Rage turns (6s/turn). Frenzied Garou cannot use Gifts
  or spend Gnosis.

SYNTAX
  +frenzy                       Show your current frenzy state.
  +frenzy/list                  List triggers and difficulties.
  +frenzy/check <trigger>       Roll Willpower vs trigger.
  +frenzy/calm <target>         Staff: end target's frenzy.
  +frenzy/force <tgt>=<state>   Staff: force berserk|fox|calm.

TRIGGERS
  humiliation 7   taunt 8   full-moon 6
  wound 7         surprise 6   vampire 7

RESULTS
  Botch           Immediate berserk frenzy.
  0 successes     Frenzy of the rolled type (default berserk).
  1+ successes    Resisted; stay calm.

  /check rolls Willpower minus your current wound penalty (min 1)
  against the trigger difficulty. The penalty is shown in the label.

See also: +help wod20th (overview), +help rage
