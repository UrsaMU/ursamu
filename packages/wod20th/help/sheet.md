+SHEET

  Display a character sheet.

SYNTAX
  +sheet [<target>]

  With no argument, shows your own sheet.
  Staff can view any player's sheet with +sheet <name>.
  Staff view includes staff notes and the stat audit log.

EXAMPLES
  +sheet           View your own sheet.
  +sheet Alice     View Alice's sheet (staff only).

SEE ALSO: +help stat, +help wod20th
