BEAST -- Frenzy and Rötschreck

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Resist with Self-Control (frenzy) or Courage (Rötschreck).
  Failure enters frenzy or flight.

SYNTAX
  +beast                    Show Beast state.
  +beast/list               Triggers and difficulties.
  +beast/frenzy <trigger>   Self-Control vs frenzy.
  +beast/fear <trigger>     Courage vs Rötschreck.
  +beast/calm <target>      (Staff) Clear Beast state.

NOTES
  Brujah: +2 frenzy difficulty. Hunger checks at Blood 0.

EXAMPLES
  +beast/frenzy hunger
  +beast/fear fire

SEE ALSO: +help blood, +help hazard, +help torpor, +help vtm
