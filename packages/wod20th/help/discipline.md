DISCIPLINE -- Kindred powers

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Invoke L1-5 powers. Opposed powers roll vs target Willpower.
  Thaumaturgy and Necromancy use paths: your primary path equals
  your school dots; secondary paths cap at school-1 until school 5.

SYNTAX
  +discipline               List discs and flags.
  +discipline/powers        Actives you know (* = target).
  +discipline/info <name>   Disc or power blurb.
  +discipline/primary [path]  View or set your primary path.
  +discipline/use <power>   Self, toggle, or shape.
  +discipline/use <p> <tgt> Opposed or damage.
  +discipline/use Celerity [n]  Extra actions this turn.

PATHS
  Thaumaturgy defaults to Path of Blood; Necromancy to Sepulchre.
  Buy secondary paths with XP: +xp The Green Path (new 7, then x4).

NOTES
  Potence/Fortitude passive. Claws: Brawl agg +1. Clan L1-5.

EXAMPLES
  +discipline/primary The Lure of Flames
  +discipline/use lure-candle
  +discipline/use Command Alice
