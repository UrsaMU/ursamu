PACK -- Garou pack membership and totems

  A pack is a Garou social unit (canon WtA). The alpha leads, invites
  members, and may disband the pack. Other splats cannot form or join
  packs. Totem is bound by staff once OOC alignment is confirmed.

SYNTAX
  +pack                            Show your pack or pending invites.
  +pack/list                       List all packs.
  +pack/who <name>                 Show a named pack.
  +pack/create <name>              Form a new pack; you become alpha.
  +pack/invite <player>            (Alpha) Invite a player; sends mail.
  +pack/accept [<pack>]            Accept an invite. Required when you
                                   have more than one pending.
  +pack/decline [<pack>|all]       Decline. Required when more than one.
  +pack/leave                      Leave your pack.
  +pack/disband                    (Alpha) Disband the pack.
  +pack/totem <pack>/<spirit>=<n>  (Staff) Bind a totem and rating.

NOTES
  Accepting any invite joins that pack and clears ALL other pending
  invites -- you can only belong to one pack at a time. Invites also
  arrive as in-game @mail with usage hints.
