+CHARGEN/STEPS

See also: +help chargen (overview)

FLOW
  1  Sub-template  breed/auspice/tribe (or clan).
  2  Concept       name, concept, age, nature,
                   demeanor.
  3  Attributes    /priority, then set each 1-5.
  4  Abilities     /priority, then ability dots.
  5  Advantages    backgrounds + gifts required;
                   merits/flaws optional.
  6  Freebies      spend pts OR +chargen/done.

FINISH (two different commands)
  +chargen/done     Close freebie step only.
                    Does NOT notify staff.
  +chargen/submit   Send sheet to staff queue.
                    Not a +job. Wait for approve.

SEE ALSO: +help chargen/syntax, +help sheet
