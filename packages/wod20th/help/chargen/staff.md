+CHARGEN/STAFF

See also: +help chargen (overview)

Staff-only switches (admin+):

SYNTAX
  +chargen/queue                 Pending applications.
  +chargen/approve <player>      Approve a sheet.
  +chargen/deny <p>=<reason>     Deny with reason.
  +chargen/note <p>=<text>       Staff note on a sheet.
  +chargen/staffreset <player>   Wipe another chargen.

EXAMPLES
  +chargen/queue
  +chargen/approve Alice
  +chargen/deny Bob=Need more concept detail.

SEE ALSO: +help chargen, +help sheet
