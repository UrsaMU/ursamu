MOOT PHASES (W20 pp. 78-79)

See also: +help moot (overview)

The four canon phases of a sept moot, plus the non-canonical bookends
"scheduled" (before opening) and "closed" (after the revel).

OPENING HOWL
  The Master of the Howl summons the Garou and calls down spirits. A
  short, ritual phase that marks the moot's start.

INNER SKY
  Theurges open the Umbral curtain. The sept communes with its totem
  and the local spirits.

CRACKING THE BONE
  The business of the sept. Litany charges are heard, formal Challenges
  are issued, news is shared, judgments are passed. Renown awards
  delivered via +moot/award belong here.

REVEL
  Howling, songs, dancing, and -- canonically -- a hunt. Renown awards
  may continue. Closing the moot pays +0.5 temp Honor to every attendee.
