GHOUL -- Vitae thralls

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Mortal splat, Blood pool 2, bond to domitor. Monthly vitae
  or face withdrawal (lose Disciplines).

SYNTAX
  +embrace/ghoul <mortal>   (Staff) Create ghoul (you).
  +embrace/ghoul <dom> <t>  (Staff) Named domitor.
  +embrace/vitae <ghoul>    Domitor feeds 1 BP vitae.
  +embrace/status [<ghoul>] Dependency clock and discs.
  +embrace/disc <g>=<Disc>  (Staff) Set Discipline 1.
  +embrace/withdraw <ghoul> (Staff) Force withdrawal.
  +embrace/ungoul <ghoul>   (Staff) Clear ghoul state.

EXAMPLES
  +embrace/ghoul Bob
  +embrace/vitae Bob

SEE ALSO: +help embrace, +help bond, +help blood, +help vtm
