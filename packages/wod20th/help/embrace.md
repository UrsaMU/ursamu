EMBRACE -- Create Kindred (staff)

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Turn an approved mortal into a neonate Kindred.
  Generation = sire + 1 (capped 15).

SYNTAX
  +embrace <mortal>         Sire = your Kindred sheet.
  +embrace <sire> <mortal>  Named sire and childe.

NOTES
  Childe inherits clan/sect. Malkavians gain a derangement.
  Nosferatu Appearance 0. Tremere take a bond step to sire.
  For ghouls see +help ghoul.

EXAMPLES
  +embrace Alice
  +embrace Prince Bob

SEE ALSO: +help ghoul, +help bond, +help blood, +help vtm
