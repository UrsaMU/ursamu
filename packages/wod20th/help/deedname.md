DEEDNAME -- Garou Deed Name

  Your deed name is shown in place of your login name to onlookers
  while you are in any non-homid form (Glabro, Crinos, Hispo, Lupus).
  Your login name is unchanged -- pages, targeting, and the +sheet
  still use it. Reserves the slot so nothing else can claim your name.

SYNTAX
  +deedname                    Show your current deed name.
  +deedname <value>            Set your deed name.
  +deedname/clear              Remove your deed name.
  +deedname <target>=<value>   (Staff) Set on another character.

EXAMPLES
  +deedname Storms-the-River
  +deedname/clear
  +deedname Karuk=Bloodclaw    (staff)

NOTES
  Deed names are limited to 40 characters. The change takes effect
  immediately on your next +shift or any visible room action.
