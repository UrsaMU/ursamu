HAZARD -- Fire and sunlight

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Apply aggravated from flame or sun. Then resist Rötschreck.

SYNTAX
  +hazard                   List kinds and defaults.
  +hazard/fire <tgt> [=n]   Flame (default 1 agg).
  +hazard/sun <tgt> [=n]    Sunlight (default 2 agg).

NOTES
  Players target me only. Staff may name anyone.
  Setites take extra sunlight damage.
  Follow with +beast/fear fire or sunlight.

EXAMPLES
  +hazard/fire me
  +hazard/sun me=3

SEE ALSO: +help beast, +help blood, +help hurt, +help vtm
