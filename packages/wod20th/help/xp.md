XP -- Experience Points

  See also: +help wod20th (overview)

SYNTAX
  +xp                          View your XP balance.
  +xp/spend <trait>            Spend XP to raise a trait by 1.
  +xp/gift <name>              Spend XP to learn a new gift.
  +xp/rite <slug>              Spend XP to learn a new rite.
  +xp/cost <trait>             Preview an XP cost.
  +xp/preview <trait>          Alias for /cost.
  +xp/log                      View your XP history.
  +xp/award <p>=<n> <reason>   Award XP (staff only).
  +xp/set <p>=<n> <reason>     Override XP total (staff only).

EXAMPLES
  +xp/spend Strength           Raise Strength 1 dot.
  +xp/gift Razor Claws         Learn a level-1 gift.
  +xp/rite rite-of-cleansing   Learn a level-1 rite.
  +xp/preview Brawl            Show cost without spending.

SEE ALSO: +help wod20th, +help chargen, +help gift, +help rite
