TITLE -- Earned Garou titles

  Garou prize titles. A title is earned ONCE when its criterion crosses
  (5 banes killed, 3+ scars borne, etc.), at which point a one-shot
  temp-renown reward is granted. Re-earning is a no-op. Titles do not
  decay; staff prunes via +title/revoke.

SYNTAX
  +title                       Show your earned titles.
  +title/list                  List the title catalog.
  +title/info <slug>           Show one title's criterion and reward.
  +title/sheet [<target>]      Show another character's titles.
  +title/award <tgt>=<slug>    (Staff) Grant a title manually.
  +title/revoke <tgt>=<slug>   (Staff) Remove a title (no renown refund).

EXAMPLES
  +title
  +title/info bane-killer
  +title/award Alice=spiral-breaker

SEE ALSO: +help npc, +help renown, +help sheet
