WIELD -- Ready and sheathe weapons

  +wield readies a weapon you are carrying for combat. +sheathe puts
  it away. You may wield at most two weapons at once.

SYNTAX
  +wield <item>
  +sheathe <item>

EXAMPLES
  +wield sword
  +wield silver klaive
  +sheathe sword

NOTES
  Only items with kind=weapon may be wielded. Use @eq on the item to
  set weaponType, damage, damageType, silver, etc.

SEE ALSO: +help eq, +help wear, +help attack, +help wod20th
