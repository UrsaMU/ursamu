CAERN -- Sept and caern administration

  A caern is a sacred node of spiritual power -- the heart of a sept.
  Staff creates and binds it to a room; alphas claim it for their pack;
  guardians (warders) and named positions form the leadership circle.

SYNTAX
  +caern                                Show your sept's caern, or list.
  +caern/list                           List all caerns.
  +caern/info <name>                    Show a named caern.
  +caern/roster [<name>]                Roster: rank, auspice, breed, role.
  +caern/create <name>=<level>=<type>   (Staff) Create a caern (1-5).
  +caern/setroom <name>=<roomId>        (Staff) Bind to a room (must be one).
  +caern/claim <name>                   (Alpha) Claim for the pack.
  +caern/release <name>                 (Alpha) Release the pack's claim.
  +caern/guardian <name>=<player>       (Alpha/Staff) Name a warder.
  +caern/position <c>=<player>=<pos>    (Alpha/Staff) Assign a position.
  +caern/unposition <c>=<player>        (Alpha/Staff) Clear a position.
  +caern/disband <name>                 (Staff) Destroy an unclaimed caern.

POSITIONS
  Free-form text. Canon examples: Master of the Rite, Talesinger,
  Truthcatcher, Caller of the Wyld, Gatekeeper, Den Parent, Wyrm Foe.
