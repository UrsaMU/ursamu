SCAR -- Permanent battle scars

  A Garou who survives Aggravated damage that fills the Incapacitated
  slot earns a permanent battle scar (W20 p.297). Scars do not heal
  via regen and confer Glory for bearing them. Only staff removes one.

SYNTAX
  +scar                        List your own scars.
  +scar/list                   Same as +scar.
  +scar/list <target>          List another character's scars.
  +scar/clear <target>=<slug>  (Staff) Remove one scar by slug.

EXAMPLES
  +scar                        Show your scar sheet.
  +scar/list Alice             Show Alice's scars.
  +scar/clear Alice=missing-eye  Staff removes that scar.

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help regen, +help renown
