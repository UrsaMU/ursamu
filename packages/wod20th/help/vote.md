VOTE -- Reward another character for great RP

  A small trickle of XP per vote. Caps below stop circle-jerking.

SYNTAX
  +vote <target>=<reason>       Cast a vote.
  +vote/all [<reason>]          Vote every eligible player in scene.
  +vote/log                     Show your last 10 votes cast.
  +vote/status                  Daily cap + per-pair cooldowns.

CAPS
  Per vote:    +0.20 XP (5 votes = 1 XP)
  Daily cap:   10 votes per 24h per voter (safety ceiling)
  Per target:  max 3 votes for any one person per 7 days
  Reason:      required, at least 10 characters
  Scene gate:  target must have posed at least 3 times here

EXAMPLES
  +vote Storms=Phenomenal scene at the caern with the spirits.
  +vote/all Great session everyone, thank you.

NOTES
  Both characters must be approved. You cannot vote for yourself.
  Pose counts reset when staff resets the room/scene.

SEE ALSO: +help xp, +help wod20th
