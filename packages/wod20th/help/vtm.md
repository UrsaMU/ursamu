VTM -- Vampire: the Masquerade (V20)

  Kindred night loop. Generation sets Blood max and spend/turn.

NIGHT LOOP
  +blood +feed       Vitae, heal, buff, vessels, Herd
  +discipline        L1-5 powers + blood-magic paths
  +ritual            Thaumaturgy / Necromancy rituals
  +beast +hazard     Frenzy, Rötschreck, fire/sun
  +humanity          Path rating and degeneration
  +bond +embrace     Bonds, Embrace, ghouls
  +coterie +domain   Band and city offices
  +boon +derange     Favors and derangements
  +diablerie         Amaranth (Incap or torpor victim)
  +stake +torpor     Heart-stake and death-sleep
  +sheet +chargen    Sheet and character generation
