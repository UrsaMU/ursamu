HUMANITY -- Path rating and degeneration

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Paths (Humanity, Night, Metamorphosis, Blood, Honorable
  Accord). Sins below rating force a virtue check (diff 8).

SYNTAX
  +humanity                 Show path and rating.
  +humanity/sins            Hierarchy for your path.
  +humanity/paths           List Paths of Enlightenment.
  +humanity/path <name>     Adopt a Path.
  +humanity/check <1-10>    Virtue vs sin level.
  +humanity/set <n> <tgt>   (Staff) Set rating 0-10.

EXAMPLES
  +humanity/path Night
  +humanity/check 4
