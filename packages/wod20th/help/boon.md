BOON -- Kindred favors

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Record debts between Kindred. Weights: trivial, minor,
  major, life.

SYNTAX
  +boon                       Your open boons.
  +boon/owe <who>=<weight> <reason>
                              Record that you owe them.
  +boon/pay <id>              Creditor (or staff) marks paid.
  +boon/void <id>             (Staff) Void a boon.
  +boon/weights               List weight tiers.

EXAMPLES
  +boon/owe Rook=minor saved my hide
  +boon
  +boon/pay 21721284
  +boon/weights
