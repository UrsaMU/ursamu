COMBO GIFTS

  Named maneuvers that fuse two or more known gifts into one effect.
  You must already know every prereq gift before you can learn a combo.

SYNTAX
  +combo                      List combo gifts you know.
  +combo/list                 Browse the full catalog.
  +combo/info <slug>          Details and prereq check for one combo.
  +combo/eligible             List combos you currently qualify to learn.
  +combo/learn <slug>         Spend XP (level x 4) to learn a combo.
  +combo/use <slug>           Activate a known combo (frenzy-gated).
  +combo/forget <slug>        (Staff) Remove a combo from your list.

EXAMPLES
  +combo/info cooking-the-books     Show prereqs and effect.
  +combo/eligible                   See what you can learn right now.
  +combo/learn cooking-the-books    Pay XP, mark it learned.
  +combo/use cooking-the-books      Fire the combo.
