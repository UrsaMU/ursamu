BLOOD -- Kindred vitae pool

  See also: +help vtm (overview)

  Spend Blood for healing, Physical buffs, and Disciplines.
  Max and per-turn spend come from generation.

SYNTAX
  +blood                    Show pool, per-turn, buffs.
  +blood/spend <n>          Spend n BP (turn-capped).
  +blood/heal [<n>]         Heal n B/L (1 BP each).
  +blood/heal/agg           Heal 1 agg (5 BP).
  +blood/buff <str|dex|sta> Physical +1 scene (1 BP).
  +blood/buff/clear         End blood buffs.
  +blood/feed [<n>]         Quick regain (no vessel).
  +blood/set <n> <tgt>      (Staff) Set current Blood.

EXAMPLES
  +blood/buff strength
  +blood/heal/agg

SEE ALSO: +help feed, +help discipline, +help beast, +help vtm
