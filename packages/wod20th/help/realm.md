REALM -- Umbral Realm catalog

  Browse-only catalog of the W20 Umbral Realms (Ch 7). Garou enter the
  Penumbra via +stepside; the deeper Realms require ritual passage and
  are not free-travel destinations. Moon bridges between caerns are
  handled by +stepside/moonbridge.

SYNTAX
  +realm                 List all Realms (Near + Deep).
  +realm/list            Same as +realm.
  +realm/info <slug>     Show one Realm's description.
  +realm/near            Just the Near Umbra Realms.
  +realm/deep            Just the Deep Umbra Realms.

EXAMPLES
  +realm/info pangaea
  +realm/info battleground
  +realm/near

SEE ALSO: +help stepside, +help caern, +help spirit
