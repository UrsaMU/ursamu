+BBJOIN

Subscribe to a BBS board. Joined boards are
included in +bbnext and unread scans.

SYNTAX
  +bbjoin <#>

  Board must be readable. Already-joined
  boards report a friendly message.

EXAMPLES
  +bbjoin 2    Subscribe to board 2.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/groups, +help bbleave
