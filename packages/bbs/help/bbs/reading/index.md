+BBS/READING

Read boards, topics, and catch up on unread posts.

COMMANDS
  +bblist                  — List all accessible boards.
  +bbread [<#>[/<posts>]]  — Read boards or specific posts.
  +bbnew [<#>]             — List unread (does not mark read).
  +bbscan                  — Unread counts per joined board.
  +bbnext [<#>]            — Read your next unread post.
  +bbcatchup [<#>]         — Mark boards or posts as read.
  +bbsearch <#>/<query>    — Search messages by author or tag.
  +bbwatch <#>/<post>      — Toggle reply-watch notifications.
  +bbversion               — Package version.
  +bbhelp                  — Points at +help bbs.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs (overview)
