+BBSEARCH

Search posts on a board by author or tag.

SYNTAX
  +bbsearch <#>/<query>

  Plain query matches author name.
  `tag:<name>` matches post tags.

EXAMPLES
  +bbsearch 2/Alice       Posts by Alice.
  +bbsearch 2/tag:lore    Posts tagged lore.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/reading, +help bbtag
