+BB

Append text to your current BBS draft.

SYNTAX
  +bb <text>

  Requires an open draft from +bbpost or
  +bbreply. Each call adds a new line.
  MUSH codes are preserved in the body.

EXAMPLES
  +bb This is the first paragraph.
  +bb %r%rAnd a second paragraph.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/posting, +help bbpost
