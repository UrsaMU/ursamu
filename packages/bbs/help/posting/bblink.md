+BBLINK

Link or unlink a scene from a BBS post.
You must own the post (or be staff).

SYNTAX
  +bblink <#>/<post>[=<sceneId>]

  No sceneId clears the link.

EXAMPLES
  +bblink 2/3=abc123    Link scene abc123.
  +bblink 2/3           Remove the link.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/posting
