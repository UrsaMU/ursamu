+BBCONFIRM

Confirm deletion of a board marked with
+bbcleargroup. Deletes the board and all
posts. Staff only. Irreversible.

SYNTAX
  +bbconfirm <#>

EXAMPLES
  +bbconfirm 3    Delete board 3 and posts.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/staff, +help bbcleargroup
