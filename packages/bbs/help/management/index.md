+BBS/MANAGEMENT

Moderator commands to manage posts and boards.

COMMANDS
  +bbremove <#>/<posts>     — Delete posts.
  +bbmove <#>/<post> to <#> — Move post to another board.
  +bbsticky <#>/<post>      — Pin/unpin a post.
  +bbflag <#>/<post>=<rzn>  — Flag a post for review.
  +bbunflag <#>/<post>      — Clear flags on a post.
  +bbarchive <#>            — Toggle read-only archive mode.
  +bbmod <#>=<player>       — Add or remove a board moderator.
  +bbreview [<#>]           — List flagged posts.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs (overview)
