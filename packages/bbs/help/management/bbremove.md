+BBREMOVE

Delete your own posts. Staff and board mods
can delete any post on their board.

SYNTAX
  +bbremove <#>/<post[,post...]>

  Comma-separated list deletes several.
  Remaining posts are renumbered.

EXAMPLES
  +bbremove 2/3
  +bbremove 2/3,4,5

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/management
