+BBREVIEW

List flagged posts. Board mods see boards
they moderate; staff see all.

SYNTAX
  +bbreview [<#>]

  No args: all moderated boards.
  `<#>`: one board only.

EXAMPLES
  +bbreview      All flagged posts you can see.
  +bbreview 2    Flagged posts on board 2.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs/management, +help bbflag
