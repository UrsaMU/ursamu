+VITAE  -- Vampire Vitae pool (Blood Potency).

SYNTAX
  +vitae                         View BP and Vitae.
  +vitae/spend <n>               Spend Vitae (per-turn cap).
  +vitae/heal                    Heal 1 bashing (1 Vitae).
  +vitae/heal-lethal             Heal 1 lethal (1 Vitae).
  +vitae/blush                   Blush of Life (1 Vitae).
  +vitae/boost <str|dex|sta>     +2 Attribute scene (1 Vitae).
  +vitae/gain <n> [for <p>]      Staff: grant Vitae.
  +vitae/set <n> [for <p>]       Staff: set current.

Per-turn spend is capped by Blood Potency. Pool max follows
the VtR BP table (BP 1 = 10, BP 10 = 75).
