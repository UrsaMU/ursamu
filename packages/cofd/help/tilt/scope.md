+TILT SCOPE  -- Personal vs environmental Tilts.

PERSONAL
  One character (Arm Wrack, Blinded, Stunned…).

ENVIRONMENTAL
  Whole scene (Blizzard, Flooded, Ice…). Apply to
  each affected sheet.

BEATS
  Tilts award no Beats on resolve (core p.282).

SCENE END
  +tilt/clear sweeps. Wound-linked Tilts clear as
  damage heals, not via blanket /clear.
