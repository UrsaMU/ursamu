+GEAR WEAPONS  -- Ranged and melee mechanics.

RANGED
  Damage = bonus successes on a hit. Range −1/−2.
  Init penalty while wielded. Str below min −1.
  Shotguns 9-again; autofire bursts; crossbows slow.

MELEE
  Lethal. Dual-wield: higher Init penalty +1.
  Specials: Concealed, Grapple, Stun, Two-handed.

STAKES
  Heart −3; need 5+ damage in one attack.
