+FRUIT  -- Goblin fruit (carry, forage, eat).

SYNTAX
  +hedge/forage              Harvest (Wits+Survival).
  +hedge/fruit               List fruit you carry.
  +hedge/fruit catalog       Full fruit catalog.
  +hedge/eat <slug>          Eat one unit.

NOTES
  Fruit are real inventory objects. Leaving the
  Hedge can rot extras over your carry cap
  (based on Wyrd). Markets also sell fruit.

EXAMPLES
  +hedge/forage
  +hedge/eat amaranthine
  +market/buy faerie-peach

SEE ALSO: help hedge, help market, help spin
