+CONTRACT  -- List or invoke a Changeling Contract.

SYNTAX
  +contract                  List Contracts on sheet.
  +contract/list             Same.
  +contract/info <name>      Catalog detail.
  +contract <name>           Invoke (cost; roll if any).

RULES
  Must already know the Contract (chargen / staff).
  Costs parse Glamour and Willpower from catalog.
  Mask down (`+shift mien`): successes exceptional
  (floored at Wyrd).

EXAMPLES
  +contract
  +contract/info Mask of Superiority
  +contract Chrysalis
