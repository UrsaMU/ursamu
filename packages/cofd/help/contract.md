+CONTRACT  -- List or invoke a Changeling Contract.

SYNTAX
  +contract                      List Contracts on your sheet.
  +contract/list                 Same.
  +contract/info <name>          Catalog detail.
  +contract <name>               Invoke (pay cost; roll if any).

RULES
  You must already know the Contract (chargen / staff).
  Costs parse Glamour and Willpower from the catalog.
  While **Mask is down** (`+shift mien`), successful rolls
  count as **exceptional** (successes floored at Wyrd).

EXAMPLES
  +contract
  +contract/info Mask of Superiority
  +contract Mask of Superiority
  +contract Chrysalis
