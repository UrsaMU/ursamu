+SOCIAL DOORS  -- Door count and contested rolls.

BASE
  Doors = min(Resolve, Composure).

ROLL (+social/door)
  Manipulation + Persuasion vs Composure.
  Fail: cumulative −1. Exceptional: open 2 doors.
  Dramatic fail: maneuver ends permanently.

END
  Last door falls → subject capitulates.
