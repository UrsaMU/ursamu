+ZONE SWITCHES  -- Full switch list (builder+).

SWITCHES
  /list              Show all zones.
  /show <name>       Inspect a zone.
  /create <name>     Anchor zone to this room.
  /add <name>=ids    Add explicit room ids.
  /from-exits <name> Walk exits (cap 200 rooms).
  /populate ...      Spawn archetypes or theme mix.
  /theme <n>=theme   Set/clear theme tag.
  /flavor <n>=on|off Ambient broadcasts.
  /respawn <n>=secs  Refill spawn deficits.
  /migration on|off  Mobs may cross zones.
  /wander on|off     Start/stop wander tick.
  /destroy <name>    Delete definition (not NPCs).
