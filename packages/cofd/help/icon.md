+ICON  -- Lost pieces of self (CtL Icons).

SYNTAX
  +icon                      List your Icons.
  +icon/info <id|name>       Full detail.
  +icon/spend <id> [=note]   Burn for Glamour surge.

STAFF
  +icon/grant <p>=name[/kind[/heldBy]] [desc]
  +icon/hold <p> <id>=holder
  +icon/recover <p> <id>
  +icon/remove <p> <id>

NOTES
  Kinds: memory, skill, emotion, relationship,
  other. Spend: Glamour up to Wyrd (max 3),
  status **spent**. Recover is plot/staff.

EXAMPLES
  +icon
  +icon/spend mothers-song=defy the Huntsman
  +icon/grant Pix=First Love/emotion/Rival
