+DISTRICT  -- District traits and room limits.

SYNTAX
  +district                     Current room traits.
  +district/show [<target>]     Room or parent.
  +district/set <trait>=<val>   Set trait (−5 to 5).
  +district/type <archetype>    Set archetype.
  +district/create-parent <n>[=<arch>]

PERMISSIONS
  View: connected. Edit: owner or builder+.

TRAITS
  access, safety, information, awareness, prestige,
  stability, sizemax, securitymin, locationmin
