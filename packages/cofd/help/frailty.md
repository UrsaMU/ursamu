+FRAILTY

  List Wyrd frailties (taboos and banes, CtL p.102).

SYNTAX
  +frailty
  +frailty/iron
  +frailty/check <text>
  +sheet/set frailty=taboo: never dance at midnight
  +sheet/set frailty=bane: cold iron
  +sheet/set frailty=-<text>

RULES
  Acting against a frailty source costs 1 Willpower per
  action and −3 dice (−5 if major). Cold iron ignores fae
  armor magic and deals aggravated to changelings.

EXAMPLES
  +frailty
  +frailty/iron
  +sheet/set frailty=major bane: pure iron nails

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help sheet
