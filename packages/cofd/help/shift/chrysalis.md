+SHIFT CHRYSALIS  -- Animal form via Contract Chrysalis.

REQUIREMENTS
  Contract **Chrysalis** on sheet.
  Animals list: `+sheet/set animals=wolf,hawk`
  Slots: 2 (Beast seeming: 4).

LIMITS
  Ogre Size cap 15; others Size ≤ 7.
  Cost: 2 Glamour enter; free leave via **human**.

EFFECTS
  Physical attrs + Size + Speed from animal.
  +2 Perception on Wits+Composure (scent / sight).

EXAMPLES
  +sheet/set animals=wolf,hawk
  +shift/list animals
  +shift wolf
  +shift human
