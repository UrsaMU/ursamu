+GRAPPLE MOVES  -- Control success picks a move.

MOVES
  /break-free    End grapple (reflexive).
  /control-wpn   Seize a weapon in the grapple.
  /damage        Bashing = successes (+wpn if controlled).
  /disarm        After /control-wpn.
  /drop-prone    Both fall prone.
  /hold          No Defense until free.
  /restrain      Immobilized after /hold.
  /take-cover    Human shield vs ranged this turn.

  Exceptional control: two moves same turn.

SEE ALSO: help grapple, help grapple/firearms
