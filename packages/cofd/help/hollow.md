+HOLLOW  -- Hollow Merit enhancements (CtL p.116).

SYNTAX
  +hedge/claim               Claim unclaimed Hollow.
  +hedge/hollow [list]       Enhancement catalog.
  +hedge/hollow status       This Hollow’s budget.
  +hedge/hollow <slug>       Spend free dots.
  +hedge/hollow remove <s>
  +hedge/hollow escape-to <r>
  +hedge/escape              Use Escape Route.
  +hedge/access              Easy Access (1 Glamour).

NOTES
  Rating 0–5 = enhancement budget. Slugs include
  size[-2], escape-route[-2], easy-access (3),
  home-turf (3). Full list: +hedge/hollow list.
