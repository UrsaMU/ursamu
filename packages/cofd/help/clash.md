+CLASH

  Resolve a Clash of Wills between two sheeted characters
  (CtL p.126 / CofD supernatural contests).

SYNTAX
  +clash <target> [reason]

RULES
  Each side rolls Power Stat + higher of Resolve or
  Composure. Higher successes win; ties cancel.

EXAMPLES
  +clash Alice
  +clash Bob Contract vs Contract

SEE ALSO: +help contract, +help changeling
