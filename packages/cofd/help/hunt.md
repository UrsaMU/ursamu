+HUNT

  Wild Hunt loop and Huntsman Dread Powers (CtL p.262+).

SYNTAX
  +hunt                     Status (quarry or Huntsman).
  +hunt/powers              Power catalog.
  +hunt/power <slug> [note] Activate a power.
  +hunt/track <quarry>      Advance hunt (track roll).
  +hunt/read [<quarry>]     Kindred Spirits (1G).

STAFF
  +hunt/mark <ch>=<huntsman>   Begin hunt.
  +hunt/end [<who>]            End hunt.
  +hunt/create <player>[=title]
  +hunt/grant <p>=<slug>

STAGES (progress 0–10)
  scent → trail → closing → cornered

NOTES
  Track pool Wits+Survival+Wyrd. If quarry Mask is down,
  add their Wyrd. Hedge quarry: +1. Heart of Iron: no cold
  iron fear. NPC archetype: huntsman.

SEE ALSO: +help changeling, +help fetch, +help dream
