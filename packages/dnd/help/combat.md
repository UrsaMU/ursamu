+COMBAT

Walker-backed turn combat in the current room.

SYNTAX
  +combat/start | /join | [/status] | /pass | /end

SWITCHES
  /start   Roll init for room combatants; run leading NPCs
  /join    Join an open encounter
  /status  Show initiative and HP bands
  /pass    End your turn; NPC AI until next PC
  /end     Resolve the encounter

EXAMPLES
  +combat/start
  +combat/status
  +attack Orc
  +combat/pass

SEE ALSO: +help attack, +help cast, +help npc
