+DEATH / UNDERWORLD

When a PC **dies** (not merely 0 HP), the spirit goes to
the Grey Veil and a corpse keeps their gear.

DYING (0 HP)
  Unconscious, out of the fight. When combat ends with
  the party down, death saves roll automatically (up to
  stable or dead). You can also +ds while dying.

DEATH
  3 failed saves (or massive damage). Corpse left where
  you fell; spirit goes to the Grey Veil.

COME BACK
  Ally: +res <corpse> — full raise, gear restored.
  Alone: +res me — home, lose gear on corpse, ~10%
  XP and ~10% of coin purse (all denominations).

COMMANDS
  +ds · +ds/status · +res me · +res <corpse>

SEE ALSO: +help deathsave, +help res, +help hp
