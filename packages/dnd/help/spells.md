+SPELLS

Browse the SRD spell catalog used by **+cast**.

SYNTAX
  +spells
  +spells <level|name>
  +spells/known
  +spells/info <name>

NOTES
  Levels 0–3 in the catalog. Cast only spells you know
  (`+spells/known`).

EXAMPLES
  +spells
  +spells 1
  +spells fireball
  +spells/known
  +cast Fireball on Orc

SEE ALSO: +help cast, +help sheet
