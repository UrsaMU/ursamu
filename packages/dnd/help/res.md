+RES

Raise the dead — full or cheap self-return.

SYNTAX
  +res <corpse>     Full raise (ally at body)
  +res me           Cheap self-res from the Veil
  +res <player>     Staff full raise

FULL RAISE
  Spirit returns at the corpse with gear and 1 HP.

CHEAP SELF (+res me)
  Home start, 1 HP. Gear stays on the corpse.
  Lose about 10% XP and 10% of your **coin purse**
  (pp/gp/ep/sp/cp — not only gold).

EXAMPLES
  +res corpse of Alice
  +res me
  +res #88

SEE ALSO: +help death, +help deathsave
