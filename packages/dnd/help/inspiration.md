+INSPIRATION

Heroic inspiration for advantage on a check or attack.

SYNTAX
  +inspiration [<player>]
  +inspiration/give [<player>]
  +inspiration/take [<player>]
  +roll/insp <check>

NOTES
  Staff (or editors) grant inspiration with /give. Spend it
  with **+roll/insp** on a check, save, attack, or init.

EXAMPLES
  +inspiration/give Alice
  +roll/insp Stealth
  +roll/insp/adv save Wisdom

SEE ALSO: +help roll, +help condition
