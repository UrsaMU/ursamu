+STAFFKIT

Staff tools for chargen, XP, delves, and spawns.

  +staffkit
  +staffkit/cgen
  +staffkit/approve <player>
  +staffkit/xp <player>=<n>

  +staffkit/encounter <template>
  +staffkit/skins
  +staffkit/towns
  +staffkit/caravans

EXAMPLES
  +staffkit/towns
  +staffkit/caravans
  +dnd/world/seed
