+CARAVAN

Escort wagons along seeded roads for pay and rep.

SYNTAX
  +caravan
  +caravan/take <slug>
  +caravan/leg
  +caravan/deliver
  +caravan/status
  +caravan/drop

NOTES
  Each /leg may ambush. Clear foes, then /leg again.
  Deliver when legs complete for XP, gp, and rep.

EXAMPLES
  +caravan/take flour-run
  +caravan/leg
  +caravan/deliver

SEE ALSO: +help road, +help bounty, +help rep
