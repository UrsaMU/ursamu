+INVENTORY

List and equip D&D gear. Alias: +inv.

SYNTAX
  +inventory
  +wield <weapon> | +unwield <weapon>
  +wear <armor|shield> | +remove <item>

NOTES
  One weapon and one armor/shield set. Equipped gear
  cannot be dropped or given. AC follows armor + Dex.

EXAMPLES
  +inv
  +wield Greatsword
  +wear Scale Mail

SEE ALSO: +help item, +help attack
