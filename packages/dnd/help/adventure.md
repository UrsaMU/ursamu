+ADVENTURE

Procedural dungeons/camps. Foe counts scale with **party
size** (PCs in the room + your hirelings).

SYNTAX
  +adv
  +adv/delve <skin>
  +adv/leave
  open <chest>
  use <altar|campfire>

NOTES
  Stand with allies and hirelings, then delve. Boss room
  holds the end chest. Hire: +hire

EXAMPLES
  +hire guard
  +adv/delve goblin-warren
  open Stolen Chest

SEE ALSO: +help hire, +help chest, +help combat
