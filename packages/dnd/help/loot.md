+LOOT

Take gear and gold from corpses — or sweep the room.

SYNTAX
  +loot
  +get
  +loot <corpse>
  +loot all

NOTES
  Bare +loot grabs every corpse and gold pile here. GP goes
  to your purse. Sell extras in town with +sell.

EXAMPLES
  +loot
  +loot corpse of goblin
  +sell Longsword

SEE ALSO: +help kill, +help chest, +help money
