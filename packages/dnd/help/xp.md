+XP

View experience points; staff award or set totals.

SYNTAX
  +xp [<player>]
  +xp/award <n> [=player]
  +xp/set <n> [=player]

NOTES
  Level up with **+level** when you meet the PHB threshold.
  Only staff may /award or /set.

EXAMPLES
  +xp
  +xp/award 300=Alice
  +xp/set 900
  +level

SEE ALSO: +help level, +help sheet
