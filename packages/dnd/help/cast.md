+CAST

Cast a known spell from the SRD catalog. In combat, must
be your turn; ends your turn after resolving.

SYNTAX
  +cast <spell>
  +cast <spell> on <target>

NOTES
  Slot level matches the spell (cantrips free). Healing,
  attack, auto-hit, and save spells resolve from the table.
  Concentration spells set your conc; damage may break it.

EXAMPLES
  +cast Cure Wounds
  +cast Guiding Bolt on Orc
  +cast Hex on Bandit
  +cast Magic Missile on Goblin

SEE ALSO: +help spells, +help condition, +help combat
