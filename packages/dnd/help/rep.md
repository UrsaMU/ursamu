+REP

Faction standing, titles, shop and hire discounts.

SYNTAX
  +rep
  +rep/unlocks
  +rep/set <faction>=<n> [<player>]   (staff)

NOTES
  Shop +5/10/25 → 5/10/20% off. Hire discounts at titles.
  Earn: +bounty/turnin, +caravan/deliver.

EXAMPLES
  +rep
  +rep/unlocks

SEE ALSO: +help bounty, +help caravan, +help hire
