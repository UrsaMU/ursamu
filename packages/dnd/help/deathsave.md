+DEATHSAVE

Death saving throws at 0 HP. Alias: +ds.

SYNTAX
  +deathsave | +ds
  +ds/status
  +ds/stabilize [<player>]

NOTES
  3 successes → stable. 3 failures → **dead**
  (corpse + underworld). Nat 20 → 1 HP and wake.

EXAMPLES
  +ds
  +ds/status
  +ds/stabilize Bob

SEE ALSO: +help death, +help res, +help hp
