+LEVEL

Spend XP to gain a level. ASI/feat at 4/8/12/16/19.

SYNTAX
  +level
  +level <class>
  +level/status
  +level/asi <ability> [2|ability]
  +level/feat <name>
  +level/spell <name>

EXAMPLES
  +level
  +level/status
  +level/asi strength 2
  +level/asi dex con
  +level/feat Alert
  +level/spell fireball

SEE ALSO: +help xp, +help sheet, +help cg
