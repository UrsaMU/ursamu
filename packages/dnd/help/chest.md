+CHEST

Open adventure treasure containers in the room.

SYNTAX
  open <name>
  +chest [open] <name>

NOTES
  Prefer the core **open** verb. Each chest rolls a loot
  table once. GP goes to your purse; gear to inventory.
  `+chest` is a thin alias.

EXAMPLES
  open Stolen Chest
  open Burial Coffer
  +money

SEE ALSO: +help adventure, +help loot
