+cg  -- Guided character generation.

SYNTAX
  +cg
  +cg/set <trait>=<value>
  +cg/back
  +cg/next
  +cg/submit
  +cg/reset

STAGES
  Stage 1: Basic Profile
    Set your class, species, and background.
    - Examples:
      +cg/set class=Wizard
      +cg/set species=Elf
      +cg/set background=Sage

  Stage 2: Ability Scores
    Set your Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma.
    - Examples:
      +cg/set strength=15
      +cg/set intelligence=16
      +cg/set wisdom=10

  Stage 3: Completion
    Submitting finalized chargen creates your live approved character sheet.
    - Command: +cg/submit
