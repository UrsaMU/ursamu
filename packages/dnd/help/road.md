+ROAD

Overland routes between campaign towns.

SYNTAX
  +road
  +road/go <route|town>
  +road/check
  +road/status

NOTES
  Whisperwood Road links Havenbrook gate to Millhaven.
  Walk exits on leg rooms; +road/check rolls ambushes.

EXAMPLES
  +road
  +road/go millhaven
  +road/check

SEE ALSO: +help travel, +help world, +help bounty
