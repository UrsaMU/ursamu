+HIRE

Pay gp for a companion. Hirelings join your **party size**
for +adv/delve mob scaling and follow you into sites.

SYNTAX
  +hire
  +hire <kit>
  +hire/list
  +hire/dismiss <name>

KITS
  porter 5gp · guard 25gp · scout 30gp · acolyte 40gp

EXAMPLES
  +hire guard
  +hire/list
  +adv/delve goblin-warren

SEE ALSO: +help adventure, +help money
