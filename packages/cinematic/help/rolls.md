+ROLL

Roll D10 + Attribute + Skill. A total of 9 or better succeeds.
Success Levels climb with the total (9-10 = 1 SL, 17-20 = 5 SL).

SYNTAX
  +roll <attr> [<skill>] [<+/-mod>]
  +maneuvers                   List combat maneuvers (roll/damage).

NOTES
  With no skill, the Attribute is doubled (Director's call).
  Drama Point spend (before rolling) adds +10 to one roll.

EXAMPLES
  +roll dexterity kung-fu
  +roll intelligence occultism +2
  +maneuvers

SEE ALSO: +help drama
