+SENSE

Supernatural sensing and masking (rules supplement) —
sensing reveals impressions, not sheets; Masking hides signatures.

SYNTAX
  +sense                     Read the scene ambience.
  +sense <character>         Focused examination.
  +sense undead              Necromancer search.
  +mask                      Show masking state.
  +mask/on | /off            Drop or raise the Mask (Mask 1+).
  +mask/<nature|power|master|alpha>  Toggle concealed aspect.
  +mask/set=<text>           Permitted surface impression.

NOTES
  Active sensing: Perception + Notice + 1d10 vs Willpower + Mask
  + 1d10 when masked; ties to the target. Failures never reveal
  concealed signatures.

EXAMPLES
  +sense Michelle
  +mask/on
