+DRAMA

Drama Points bend the story. Announce the spend before rolling.

SYNTAX
  +drama                       Show remaining points.
  +drama/heroic                Heroic Feat: +10 to one roll or damage.
  +drama/okay                  I Think I'm Okay: halve damage taken.

NOTES
  Plot Twist, Righteous Fury (2 pts, +5 all attacks), and Back From
  the Dead (1/5/10 pts) are Director adjudicated — ask staff.
  Points return only when the Director awards them.

EXAMPLES
  +drama
  +drama/heroic
SEE ALSO: +help rolls
